Anniversaries mark important days. The calendar highlights them and shows which anniversary it is or how many days are left. A one-time date in the future works as a countdown.

## Turning it on
Anniversaries are off by default and depend on the calendar: in Settings → Features, keep "Calendar" on, then turn on "Anniversaries" below it. Turning it off only hides them; your anniversaries are kept.

## Where to find it
- The heart button in the calendar's top bar opens the "Anniversaries" list.
- The "Anniversaries" card on the Explore page.

## Adding one
- Tap "+" at the top right of the Anniversaries list.
- Long-press a day on the calendar and choose "Add an anniversary" (future days default to Once, i.e. a countdown).
- On the preview page, "More → Make it an anniversary" fills in the diary's date and title and links that diary.

On the edit page you can set:
- Name, Date and Note;
- Repeat: **Yearly** (the same date every year), **Lunar yearly** (converted from the lunar date each year), or **Once** (just this day: it counts down before and counts up after);
- Linked diary: pick one written within 7 days of the chosen date;
- Pin: keep it at the top of the anniversary list.

## List and details
The list is ordered by the next occurrence, soonest first (pinned ones on top, past countdowns at the bottom), with the days left on the right. Tap one for its details: the countdown card, Started / Repeat / Note, the linked diary, and "On this day over the years" with the diaries written on that day each year. Edit from the top right; "More actions" lets you pin or delete. Deleting asks for confirmation, and you can still undo it from the notice afterwards.

## On the calendar
With lunar dates shown, the anniversary's name takes the lunar line of the day cell; otherwise a small heart appears at the top left of the date. Selecting the day shows its anniversary card. Turn this off under Calendar display → Sections → Anniversaries.

## Notes
- Dates that don't exist in a year move to the nearest day: Feb 29 becomes Feb 28 in common years, and lunar leap months and the 30th of a lunar month shift likewise.
- Anniversaries don't send reminder notifications.
- Deleting an anniversary doesn't affect the linked diary. If the linked diary is deleted, the detail page says so and lets you unlink it.
- Anniversaries are included in full backups.
- The AI assistant can create or delete anniversaries, for example "Add an anniversary: Mom's birthday, lunar 8/15, every year".
