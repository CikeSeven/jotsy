**Archive** tucks away diaries you don't want to see on the home page without deleting them. The **recycle bin** holds deleted diaries, which you can restore or delete permanently.

## Archiving
- On a phone, **swipe a card left** in the diary list and let go past the threshold to archive it. You can undo from the notice.
- Long-press a card to start selecting, pick several, and tap the archive button in the top bar to archive them together.
- On a computer, right-click a card and choose "Archive".
- Archived diaries no longer show in the home list, but the calendar and Explore stats still count them, and search finds them when "Include archived" is on.

## Viewing and unarchiving
Tap the archive button (box icon) in the Diaries tab's top bar to open "Archived diaries".
- On a phone, swipe a card left to unarchive it.
- Long-press to start selecting, then "Unarchive" or delete several at once.
- On a computer, right-click a card and choose "Unarchive".

## Deleting diaries
- Use "More → Delete" on the preview page, or select diaries on the home page and tap delete. After you confirm, they move to the recycle bin, and you can undo right away from the notice.
- On a computer you can also right-click a card and choose "Delete", or press Delete while reading in the two-pane view.
- When deleting a notebook, "Also move entries to trash" sends its entries to the recycle bin as well.

## Recycle bin
Go to Settings → Recycle Bin (in the "Organize" group).
- The most recently deleted come first, each with the diary's date and when it was deleted.
- Tap or long-press to tick diaries; "Select all" is at the top right. Ticking shows "Restore" and "Permanent delete" at the bottom.
- Restoring needs no confirmation; the diary returns to its original place and notebooks.
- Permanent delete asks for confirmation and can't be undone.
- The recycle bin is never emptied automatically; diaries stay until you restore or permanently delete them.
- Entries from private notebooks only show as "Entry in a private notebook" in the recycle bin.

## Notes
- Swipe to archive is on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Features → Swipe to archive; you can still archive by selecting. The switch and the gesture exist only on phones; computers use the right-click menu.
- The AI assistant can archive, unarchive and move diaries to the recycle bin, but it can't delete permanently. It can open the recycle bin for you.
