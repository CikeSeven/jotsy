Background & Material lets you put a background image or color behind the app and turn components such as top bars, sheets and dialogs into frosted glass that lets the background show through. By default there's no background and every component is Classic (solid).

## Where
- Settings → Appearance → Background & Material. A live preview sits at the top (switch between light and dark preview), with two tabs below: **Background** and **Glass**.

## Setting a background
1. In the area chips at the top of the Background tab, pick an area: Global, Diaries, To-dos, Calendar, Explore, Settings, Editor, Preview, Other.
2. With **Global** selected, set the type under "Global background" to **None**, **Color** or **Image**. For a color, pick it in "Background color"; for an image, tap **Choose image**, then **Change** or **Remove** it later.
3. Every other area uses **Follow global** by default. You can also choose **None** (the area keeps the theme color) or **Custom** (give the area its own color or image). Areas that don't follow global show a small dot after their name.
4. For an image background, the **Parameters** group has **Focus** (tap or drag on the image to choose which part stays in view when it's cropped), **Blur**, **Light mode veil** and **Dark mode veil** (a layer over the image in light or dark theme that keeps text readable).

What each area covers: Diaries, To-dos, Calendar and Explore are the matching home tabs; Settings is the Settings page and all its sub-pages; Editor is the pages for writing, publishing and editing templates; Preview is the diary preview page (only shown when the preview page's paper follows the theme); Other is search, notebooks, anniversaries, drafts, archive, recycle bin and the remaining pages.

## Follow from
- An area set to its own image has a **Follow from** row at the end of its group, where you choose Global or another area as the source.
- The Image and Parameters groups below each start with a switch ("Use the image from …" and "Use the parameters from …"). When on, the area uses whatever the source currently shows and updates when the source changes. Turn it off and the area's own image and parameters are still there.

## Glass components
- Glass tab → **Component style**: **Classic** keeps components solid; **Glass** lets the background and content show through them.
- When any component uses glass, a **Glass texture** group appears: Blur, Opacity, Tint (Neutral / Theme / Custom, with the color picked in "Glass color" for Custom) and Outline.
- **Per component**: set each of six component types to Follow, Classic or Glass: Page top bars, Editor toolbar, Bottom sheets, Dialogs, Cards & groups, Controls & toasts. For example, keep everything Classic and make only Dialogs glass.
- Glass for the home bottom bar and top bars, and for diary cards, isn't set here but in Navigation bar style and Diary card style (the Per component group ends with entries to both); see "Diary card, preview page and navigation bar styles".

## Notes
- Cards, groups and page top bars only blur when the background is an image; with a color background or none they are simply translucent.
- When the system's high contrast mode is on, glass automatically turns opaque.
- Background & Material settings and background images are included in full backups.
- The AI assistant can't change backgrounds or materials for you, but it can open this page.
