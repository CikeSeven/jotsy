The Calendar tab shows your diaries by date. It's on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Features (anniversaries turn off with it).

## Basics
- Swipe left or right, or tap the arrows beside the month, to change months. Tap the month title for the **year view**, then tap a month there to go back.
- Tap a day to see its diaries below, plus "On this day" (entries from the same date in other years). With those features on, the day's anniversaries and to-dos show too.
- Top-bar buttons: Anniversaries (heart, appears when anniversaries are on), Filter, Calendar display, Back to today.
- Below the grid is the monthly summary: entries, days written, words, most frequent mood and longest streak.

## Writing for a day
- Tap a past day with no diary, then tap "Write for this day".
- Long-press any day and choose "Write for this day"; future days also offer "Write a time capsule". The long-press panel also has the mood of the day (see "Moods and the mood of the day"), Add a to-do and Add an anniversary.
- On a phone, when you write for a past day the editor lists photos taken that day for you to insert (after you allow photo access).

## Calendar display
Tap "Calendar display" in the top bar; changes apply right away:
- Day cells: Dots, Mood, Heat, Photos or Tags.
- Badges: entry count, weather, sealed time capsules.
- Lunar calendar and Public holidays (China): Language default, Show or Hide. Language default shows them in Chinese and hides them in English.
- Week starts on, Highlight weekends, Week numbers, Always show 6 weeks, and whether it opens in Month, Week or the last used view.
- Sections: switches for Monthly summary, On this day, Anniversaries, Mood of the day, Show to-dos and more.

## Public holidays
When shown, days off in mainland China get "休" (off) at the top left of the date and make-up workdays get "班" (work); weekend shading follows them. The data comes from the yearly holiday schedule published by China's State Council and only covers years announced before this version was released; later dates show as ordinary weekends.

## Filtering
The funnel button opens "Filter calendar", with Mood, Tags and Notebooks. Any choice within a group matches; when several groups are set, an entry must match all of them. Day cells, the monthly summary, the year view and range stats only count matching entries.

## Range stats
Long-press the first day, choose "Select a range from here", then tap the last day. The summary below the grid switches to stats for that range. Tap "Save as notebook" to turn the range into a notebook, or × to exit range selection.

## Year view
One small square per day, colored by mood, entries or words. You can share it as an image (on Linux it saves the image instead).

## Notes
- Sealed time capsules only show a lock badge on the calendar and reveal nothing else.
- Entries in hidden or locked notebooks don't appear on the calendar, and the filter doesn't list those notebooks or smart notebooks.
- The AI assistant can look up a day on the calendar, for example "What did I write on October 1?".
