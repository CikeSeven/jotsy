Comments are notes you leave on an entry when you reread it. They line up by date at the end of the entry like annotations, and the entry itself stays as written. A year later, for example, you can add what you think now.

## Turning comments on

Comments are off by default. Turn on Comments in Settings → Features and the entries for it appear. Turning it off again hides them; your comments are kept and come back when you turn it on. With the AI assistant on, you can also ask it to "turn on comments".

## Writing a comment

1. Open an entry's preview page, scroll to the end and tap "Write a comment…", or tap More → Write a comment.
2. In the sheet, pick a mood if you like (optional) and write up to 1000 characters.
3. Tap "Add photos" at the bottom left to attach up to 9 photos. A comment can be photos only, with no text.
4. Tap Save.

When editing an existing entry, the comments section is at the end of the page too, and More also has Write a comment.

## Viewing and managing

- Comments form a timeline from oldest to newest, each marked "N days later" (or months or years); ones written the same day say "Same day". With more than 5, the middle ones fold away; tap "Show N more".
- Photos are laid out as a collage. Tap any one to open the image viewer and swipe through them.
- Long-press a comment (right-click on desktop) to edit, copy or delete it; a deletion can be undone. Edited comments are marked "Edited".

## Elsewhere in the app

- Diary cards and the preview page's details show the comment count. Tap "N comments" in the details to jump to the comments. To hide the count, turn off "Comment count" in the diary card style and preview page style.
- Search also matches comment text; entries found only through a comment show that comment underneath.
- Explore has a "Recent comments" card.
- When exporting, you can tick "Include comments".
- With the AI assistant on, an AI reflection can be saved with "Save as comment", and you can ask the AI assistant to add a comment to an entry.

## Good to know

- Sealed time capsules and entries in the recycle bin have no comments section; archived entries can still be commented on.
- Shared text and long images don't include comments.
- Comments are included in full backups and sync to your other devices over LAN sync.
