Diary links let you mention another entry or notebook in the body and get there with one tap. The entry you mention lists who mentioned it. There are two ways: an inline link for a passing mention in a sentence, or a card when you want it to stand out.

## Inserting an inline link

- **With @**: type @ at the start of a line or after a space. Suggestions appear by the cursor in two groups, Notebooks and Diaries. Keep typing a diary title or notebook name to filter (Chinese notebook names also match pinyin), then tap one to insert the link. The link text starts as the title or notebook name; you can change it, and it doesn't change when the target is renamed.
- **With the Link button** on the toolbar (Ctrl / ⌘ + K on desktop): switch between Web, Diaries and Notebooks at the top of the dialog, then search and pick on the Diaries or Notebooks page. With text selected, the link is added to it; without a selection, the name is inserted. With the cursor inside an existing link, you can change it or tap "Remove link".

## Inserting a card

Choose "Diary card" or "Notebook card" in the slash menu, then pick the target. A diary card shows the entry's live title, date, excerpt and picture; a notebook card shows the cover and entry count. While editing, tap a card to Open it, "Convert to inline link" or delete it.

## Opening links

- On the preview page, tap a link or card to open it.
- While editing, a single tap only selects the link text; double-tap (or Ctrl / ⌘ + click on desktop) to open it. Notebooks don't open while you're editing, so the entry you're writing isn't closed.
- If the target is in a locked notebook, you'll be asked to verify first.

## Backlinks

- At the end of the preview page text (after "Last edited") you'll see "Mentioned in N diaries", listing up to 5; tap "Show all" for more. It's hidden when nothing mentions the entry. The editor shows it at the end too.
- When a notebook is mentioned, its header gets a "Mentioned in N diaries" chip; tap it to see them.

## Good to know

- Suggestions leave out entries in private notebooks, the recycle bin and the archive, as well as the entry you're editing.
- On cards, a private entry shows only "Private diary" and a sealed time capsule shows only its title and unlock date. If the target is deleted, the card says it no longer exists.
- Linking notebooks needs notebooks turned on; when they're off, notebook links show as plain text.
- Exports and shares keep only the link text, and cards export as a single title line.
- Links in the template editor can only be web addresses.
