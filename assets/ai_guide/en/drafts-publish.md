A new entry is a draft until you publish it, and it saves automatically. Tapping Publish on the new entry page publishes it right away and opens the publish page, where you add the details.

## Drafts

- Open Drafts from the button in the Diaries page's top bar (with a badge showing how many) or from Settings → Drafts.
- Every new entry is its own draft and saves when you go back or switch apps. A draft whose content is cleared is deleted automatically, and a template that was applied but never changed doesn't count as a draft.
- With drafts waiting, New opens "Continue a draft": tap a recent draft to keep writing, or choose "View all drafts", "Start from a template" or "New blank entry".
- On the Drafts page, tap a draft to continue it, swipe left to delete, or long-press to select several (with select all) and delete them. Deletions can be undone for 4 seconds. A draft disappears once it's published.

## The publish page

When you tap + at the top right (its tooltip says Publish) of the new entry page, the entry is saved as the publish page opens, and the top bar shows "Diary published". The entry preview is at the top and the details panel below (swipe up to expand it). Changes save as you make them. From top to bottom:
- **Cover**: pick an image (optional). Without one, cards and the preview page use the first image in the body.
- **Tags**: opens "Choose tags". Search by name (Chinese names also by pinyin), create a tag if it isn't there, or switch to Alphabetical order.
- **Notebooks**: choose which notebooks it goes into (when notebooks are turned on).
- **Here and now**: place and weather. On Android, the buttons on the right fetch your location and weather, and "Address detail" chooses City, District / county, Subdistrict / town, Road or House number. Elsewhere, type them in. The clock button by the place field opens "Recent places" so you can reuse a place with one tap.
- **Publish time**: the current time by default; tap to choose another date and time.
- **Time lock**: turns the entry into a time capsule (when time capsules are turned on). See "Time capsules".
- **Mood** and **Energy**: the mood comes from your mood emojis; energy has 5 levels and isn't recorded by default.

Go back when you're done to return to the list. To change things later, open the entry, tap Edit, then tap the details bar under the title to open the details panel (it has no time lock).

## Publish new entries when going back

By default, going back from a new entry leaves it in Drafts. Turn on Settings → Editor → "Publish new diaries when going back" and going back saves it as a published entry and returns to the list:
- If you haven't written anything, it just closes;
- An entry with a time capsule still goes through the publish page to be sealed;
- If publishing fails, you'll see "Couldn't publish the diary. It's kept in Drafts."

## Good to know

- Drafts are included in full backups but not in LAN sync.
- Change mood emojis in Settings → Mood emojis, and organize tags in Settings → Tag Management. See "Moods and the mood of the day" and "Tags".
- With the AI assistant on, the editor's top-right menu has "Generate title with AI" and "Suggest tags & mood with AI". You can also ask the AI assistant to change an existing entry's publish time, mood, weather, place or tags, for example "change the mood of yesterday's entry to happy".
