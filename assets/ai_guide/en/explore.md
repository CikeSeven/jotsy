The Explore tab gathers stats, memories and life logs. It's on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Features.

## Cards
The Explore page is made of cards in four groups:
- Memories & ideas: Today, On this day, Random memory, Recap, Milestones, and AI insights when the AI assistant is on.
- Charts: Dashboard, Writing heatmap, Writing rhythm, Writing habits, Mood trend, Mood breakdown, Energy trend.
- Life log: Tags, Media gallery (a photo wall of all your images that you can zoom, save, share or open the diary from), Places, Weather.
- Linked features: Drafts (shown when you have drafts), Anniversaries, Time capsules, Notebooks, Recent comments. These cards disappear when their feature is turned off.

Only some cards are shown by default; turn on the others in the customize page.

## Stats range
"This week / This month / This year / All time" at the top switches the stats range for Dashboard, Tags, Writing habits, Mood breakdown, Places and Weather. Streaks and time span always count all diaries.

## Using the cards
- Tapping an entry in a card, or a day with entries in the heatmap, opens that diary; you can swipe between the listed entries on the preview page.
- Tapping a tag in the Tags card opens a search for that tag.
- The AI insights card shows your latest recap, with "This week's recap" and "Ask my diary".
- Long-press a card (right-click on a computer) to jump to "Card settings" or "Hide this card" (undoable).

## Customizing Explore
Open it with the "Customize Explore" button at the right of the Explore top bar, or Settings → Appearance → Explore page. A live preview sits on top (switch between "My data" and "Sample data"), with four tabs below:
- Presets: Balanced (default), Minimal, Data lover, Memories and Everything. You can also save the current layout (up to 12), copy it as a code to share, or import someone else's from the clipboard.
- Cards: drag to reorder, switch cards on or off, and tap a row to expand its options and its width on wide screens.
- Appearance: density, corner radius, title style, card surface, accent color, columns on wide screens and more.
- Range: whether to show the stats range bar, and the default range.

"Reset" in the top bar restores the default layout and can be undone.

## Notes
- Stats include archived diaries, and leave out the recycle bin, private notebooks and sealed time capsules.
- When the Explore tab is off, the "Explore page" entry in Settings → Appearance is hidden too.
- The AI assistant can count things for you as well, for example "How many words did I write this month?".
