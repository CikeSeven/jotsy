The Features page lets you choose which pages and features you use. Turning a feature off only hides all of its entries, options and automatic behavior in the app; your data is always kept, and turning it back on brings everything back as it was.

## Where
- Settings → Features (the last row of the **General** group on the Settings home page). Each row is a switch and takes effect immediately.

## Pages group
Controls which tabs appear in the bottom navigation. Diaries and Settings always stay and can't be turned off.
- **Calendar** (on by default): view diaries by month, with lunar dates, holidays and anniversaries.
- **Anniversaries** (off by default): anniversaries and countdowns, marked on the calendar. It depends on the calendar: its switch sits under Calendar and only shows while Calendar is on, and turning Calendar off hides anniversaries too.
- **To-dos** (off by default): to-dos, lists and reminders separate from your diary. Turning it off hides the To-dos tab, the To-dos row in Settings, to-dos on the calendar and the to-do entries on the preview page, and stops all reminders.
- **Explore** (on by default): stats, on this day, mood trends and photo wall. Turning it off also hides the Explore style entry on the Appearance page.

## Diary features group
- **Notebooks** (on by default): sort diaries into notebooks, with chapters and book export. If you have hidden or locked notebooks, you're asked to confirm first: their diaries stay out of sight until you turn notebooks back on.
- **Diary templates** (on by default): start new diaries from a ready-made format. When off, no template suggestions appear and nothing is applied automatically.
- **Time capsules** (on by default): write to the future; opens only when it's due. When off you can't create new capsules, but existing ones still show and still open when due.
- **Comments** (off by default): leave notes at the end of a diary when you reread it; the entry stays as written. When off, the comment section on the preview page and comment counts on cards are hidden; your comments are kept.
- **AI assistant** (off by default): write, reflect, recap and chat with your own AI API. After turning it on you also need to set up a provider in Settings → AI assistant before its entries appear.
- **Swipe to archive** (on by default, phones only): swipe a diary card left to archive it, or left in Archive to restore it. When off you can still long-press a card to multi-select and tap the archive button in the top bar. Computers don't have this switch; use the card's right-click menu instead.

## Notes
- A feature that's off also disappears from the Settings home page, for example Notebooks, To-dos, Diary templates and AI assistant. If you can't find a feature, check here whether it's turned off.
- Turning something off never deletes diaries, to-dos, anniversaries, comments or AI history. The Features settings are included in full backups.
- If you had already created anniversaries before they became off by default, Anniversaries stays on for you.
- The AI assistant can turn these features on or off for you (except the AI assistant itself), for example "turn on to-dos" or "turn off Explore". If you have hidden or locked notebooks, turning off Notebooks has to be done here yourself.
