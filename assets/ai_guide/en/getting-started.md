Jotsy is a diary that lives on your own device. There's no account to create; open it and start writing. This article walks through the home screen, your first entry, and the features that start out turned off.

## The pages in the bottom bar

The bottom bar (a side rail on wide windows) has four pages by default:
- **Diaries**: the list of all your entries, and where you start writing.
- **Calendar**: entries by month or week, with lunar dates and holidays. Long-press a day to write for it.
- **Explore**: cards for stats, On this day, mood trends, the media gallery and more.
- **Settings**: appearance, editor, data management and so on.

Diaries and Settings always stay; Calendar and Explore can be turned off. The To-dos page is off by default; turn it on and a To-dos tab appears in the bottom bar.

## What's on the Diaries page

- The search bar at the top ("Search in Jotsy") searches titles and text.
- To its right: AI assistant (only once AI is turned on and set up), Drafts (with a number badge when you have drafts), Archived diaries, and More (sort order, list or waterfall layout, card style, switch notebook).
- Below it is the tag bar. Tap a tag to show only that tag, long-press to combine several, and drag down to see all tags. With notebooks turned on, the first chip is the notebook switcher (it normally reads "All entries"); tap it to open the shelf and jump to a notebook or to "Not in a notebook".
- The New button at the bottom right writes a new entry. Inside a notebook it reads "Write in" plus the notebook's name.
- On a phone, swipe a card left to archive it, or long-press to select several (add to notebook, archive, delete, pin, set tags, export and more). On desktop, use the right-click menu.

## Write your first entry

1. Tap New. If you already have drafts, "Continue a draft" opens first; tap "New blank entry".
2. Add a title (optional) and write in the body. When the keyboard is up, the floating toolbar at the bottom lets you add bold, pictures, recordings and more.
3. Tap + at the top right (its tooltip says Publish). The entry is saved as soon as the publish page opens, and the top bar shows "Diary published". Add a cover, tags, mood, weather or place here; changes save automatically.
4. Go back and the new entry is in your list.

If you leave before publishing, the new entry is kept in Drafts automatically.

## Reading and editing

- Tap a card to open the preview page. Swipe left or right to move to the previous or next entry.
- The preview page's top bar has Font & layout, Edit and More. Edit lets you change the entry in place, laid out just like the preview. On a phone you can also double-tap the text to start editing with the cursor where you tapped.
- More has Share, Export, Add to notebook, Save as template, Page style, Delete and more. Deleted entries go to the recycle bin and can be undone.

## Features that start out off

To keep the first screens simple, these are off by default. Turn them on in Settings → Features when you need them:
- To-dos
- Anniversaries (part of the calendar, so they hide when the calendar is off)
- Comments
- AI assistant (after turning it on, set up your own provider in Settings → AI assistant)

Notebooks, diary templates, time capsules and swipe to archive are on by default and can be turned off too. Turning a feature off only hides it; your data stays and comes back when you turn it on again. Once the AI assistant is on, it can also turn features on or off and open pages for you, for example "turn on comments".

## Good to know

- Your diary is stored only on this device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it, so make regular backups in Settings → Data Management.
