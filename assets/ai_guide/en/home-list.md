The Diaries tab is the home page you see when you open the app: a search bar and a few buttons at the top, then the tag bar (see "Tags" and "Notebooks") and the diary list, with the "New" button at the bottom right.

## Layout and sorting
Tap ⋮ (More) at the right of the top bar to open the view options:
- Sort: Date updated, Date published or Title, each Ascending or Descending.
- Layout: List or Waterfall.
- Card style: "Customize card style" opens Settings → Appearance → Diary card style, where you can switch presets and adjust covers, text, colors and more; see "Diary card, preview page and navigation bar styles".
- Inside a notebook, the sort section becomes "Sort in this notebook" and only applies to that notebook.

The other top-bar buttons open Drafts and the archive (see "Archive and recycle bin"); with the AI assistant on there is also an AI assistant button.

## Pinning
Pinned diaries always stay at the top of the list with a "Pinned" mark. Long-press a card to start selecting, then tap "More actions → Pin" ("Unpin" for pinned ones). On a computer you can also right-click a card.

## Multi-select and batch actions
Long-press a diary to start selecting, then tap other cards to add or remove them. From the top bar you can:
- Add to notebook, archive or delete;
- Under "More actions": pin or unpin, set tags, export; inside a regular notebook also "Remove from this notebook" and "Move to…";
- Tap "N selected" to select every diary in the current list (including ones not loaded yet); tap again to deselect all.

On a computer, Ctrl- (⌘-) click to select several and Shift-click to select a range.

## Fast scroller
In a long list, a thin handle appears at the right edge while you scroll. Hold and drag it to jump anywhere; a bubble beside it shows the year and month you've reached (the first letter when sorted by title, the chapter when a notebook uses book order).

## Opening diaries and paging
- Tap a card to open the preview page. Swipe left or right there to move to the previous or next diary, in the order of the list you came from. The swipe switch, sensitivity and paging effect are in Settings → Appearance → Diary preview page → Paging.
- On a phone, **double-tap the text** on the preview page to jump into editing with the cursor where you tapped. Turn off "Double-tap to edit" under Diary preview page → Text.
- To edit right away when you open a diary, turn on "Open diaries in the editor" in Settings → Editor. Swiping between diaries isn't available then.

## Other
- Tap the "Diaries" tab again to scroll back to the top.
- In a wide window (computer or tablet in landscape) the page becomes a list plus reading pane; tap a card to read it on the right. Keys: ↑ / ↓ switch entries, Enter edits, Delete deletes, Esc clears the selection. On a computer, right-click a card to edit, pin, add to a notebook, archive or delete. For more mouse and keyboard tips see "Desktop on Windows and Linux".
- The AI assistant can pin or unpin diaries for you, for example "Pin yesterday's entry".
