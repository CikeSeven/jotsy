The body can hold images, videos, attachments and recordings. They're copied into the diary's own storage; the originals in your photo library are never changed or deleted.

## Inserting images, videos and attachments

- Use Insert image, Insert video and Insert attachment on the toolbar, or the same commands in the slash menu. You can pick several images or videos at once; they go in at the cursor. An attachment can be any file and opens in another app when tapped.
- On Android, Insert image and Insert video first show a small menu: "Choose from gallery" or "Take photo" / "Record video". The slash menu also has Take photo and Record video. What you shoot stays only in the diary, not in the system gallery, and the camera permission is asked the first time.
- On desktop you can drag files from your file manager straight into the body.

## Images

- While editing, tap an image in the body for a menu: View, Show as thumbnail, Resize, Copy, Save to gallery (Save as… on desktop) and Delete, plus Describe with AI when the AI assistant is on.
- **Thumbnails**: Show as thumbnail shrinks the image to a square; consecutive thumbnails sit three to a row. Tap a thumbnail and choose Show at normal size to restore its earlier size.
- **Viewing**: while reading, tap an image to open the image viewer. Pinch or double-tap to zoom, swipe to page through, pull down to close. Long-press the image or tap ⋮ at the top right to share it or save it to the gallery.
- **Long images**: images at least three times as tall as they are wide (such as long screenshots) fold in the body, showing the top with a "Long image" badge. Tap to read the whole image at screen width. To show them in full on the preview page, go to Settings → Appearance → Diary preview page → Text → Long images and choose Full (the editor always folds them).
- On Android, HEIC photos are converted to JPEG when inserted, so the desktop app can show them too.

## Videos

On phones, videos play right in the body and can go fullscreen. On Windows and Linux, tapping a video opens it in the system player. The Export video button next to a video saves it to the gallery (or to a file on desktop).

## Recordings

1. Tap Recording on the toolbar. The recording panel opens under the toolbar; the microphone permission is asked the first time.
2. Tap the microphone to start. You can pause and resume while recording, and tap the name to rename it.
3. Tap the finish button and the recording goes into the body as a card. During playback, drag along the waveform to jump anywhere.

The Linux version can't record yet, but it plays recordings.

## Photos from this day

When you write an entry for a past day (for example from the calendar), the photos and videos you took that day are listed under the title:
- The first time, tap "Allow access". Jotsy never asks for photo access on its own.
- Tap a thumbnail to preview it, tap the circle at its top right to select it, then tap Insert. All opens the full panel.
- With exactly one photo selected you can "Set as cover", or long-press a thumbnail to set or remove the cover.
- After closing the strip, open it again from "Photos from this day" in the editor's top-right menu. It works when editing existing entries too.
- To stop it appearing automatically, turn off "Show that day's photos automatically" in Settings → Editor.

## Good to know

- Photos from this day is only on phones; taking photos and videos is currently Android only.
- To see or clean up the space used by images and videos, go to Settings → Storage. See "Storage management".
