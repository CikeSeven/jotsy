A mind map is a kind of diary: it lives in the same list as your other diaries, has a date, mood, tags and notebooks, and is backed up, synced and searched along with them.

## Creating a map
- On a new diary, while the body is still empty, a "Mind map" button sits under the title. Tap it and the diary becomes a mind map; the canvas grows out of the button.
- The central topic is the diary's title. Without a title you start editing the central topic straight away.
- While the map has only its central topic, it shows "Start from a template": Brainstorm, SWOT, Book notes, Project plan, Weekly plan, Decision, Trip plan and Yearly goals, each with a fitting structure and color theme; a central topic you already wrote is kept. You can also tap "Back to text" then.
- Tap "+" in the top bar to publish, like any diary; going back keeps it in Drafts.

## Editing topics
- Tap a topic to select it, tap it again (or double-tap) to edit its text. The dock at the bottom has Subtopic, Sibling, Edit, Collapse / Expand, Style and Delete. The central topic can't be deleted.
- On a phone, a bar above the keyboard offers Subtopic, Sibling, New line and Done while you type.
- Long-press a topic to pick it up and drag it: drop it on another topic to make it a subtopic, or between two topics to put it there. A long-press without moving opens the menu. On a computer, drag with the mouse and right-click for the menu.
- A selected topic with subtopics shows a small "−" button; tap it to collapse. Collapsed topics show how many topics are hidden; tap the number to expand.
- The top bar has Undo and Redo. Double-tap empty space to fit the map on screen; the percentage at the top right offers Fit, Actual size, Zoom in and Zoom out.
- Keyboard on a computer: Tab subtopic, Enter sibling (Shift + Enter before), F2 or Space edit, Delete delete, arrow keys move between topics, Ctrl + Z / Y undo and redo, Ctrl + 0 fit, Ctrl + = / − zoom, Ctrl + 1 actual size, Ctrl + / collapse or expand, Esc deselect. Scroll to pan, Ctrl + scroll to zoom.

## Stickers, labels, notes, links and checkboxes
- Select a topic and tap Insert in the dock: Stickers (priority, progress, flags, stars, faces, symbols, or any emoji; one per kind, tap again to remove), Labels, Note, Link (a web address or one of your diaries) and Checkbox (a box to tick in front of the topic).
- When subtopics have checkboxes, the parent shows a progress ring; ticked topics fade and are struck through.
- Tap the note mark on a topic to read or edit the note, and the link mark to open the website or diary.
- Select a topic and tap Style to change its shape, fill, text color, branch color, bold / italic / strikethrough and size; Reset style undoes it all.

## Outline view
- Switch between Map and Outline at the top left. The outline is the same map as an indented list; tap a row to edit it. Enter adds a sibling, Backspace on an empty row deletes it; on a computer Tab / Shift + Tab indent and outdent and Alt + ↑ / ↓ move rows, and on a phone the same buttons sit above the keyboard.

## Copy and paste
- More (or the right-click menu) has Copy, Cut and Paste (Ctrl + C / X / V on a computer). Copied topics come back with their subtopics and styles; lines of text copied from elsewhere become topics by their indentation.

## Structure and colors
Open Style in the dock (or More → Mind map style in the top bar) to change:
- Structure: Mind map (both sides), Logic chart, Left logic chart, Org chart, Tree, Timeline, Fishbone.
- Color theme: Follow app, Rainbow, Ocean, Forest, Sunset, Sakura, Ink (black and white), Paper, Neon and Macaron, each adapting to light and dark mode.
- Lines: Curved, Straight, Elbow, Rounded. Spacing: Compact, Standard, Loose. Rainbow branches (one color per main branch) and a dot grid.

## Mind maps inside a text diary
- Type "/" in the text and choose "Mind map" to insert a map and edit it full screen. Back in the text it's a map card; one undo returns to how it was before.
- While editing, tap the card to edit the map again; long-press or right-click to edit, view full screen or delete. While reading, tap the card to view it full screen.

## Reading, filtering and exporting
- List cards show a thumbnail of the map and its main topics. The preview page shows the map large; tap it to view full screen with pan and zoom.
- Smart notebooks and search filters have "Has mind maps" under media, matching mind map diaries and diaries with a map inside.
- Search, word counts, exports and shared text include every topic; exports write the map as an outline.

## Turning it off
- Settings → Features → Mind maps (on by default). Turning it off hides the button on new diaries, "Mind map" in the "/" menu and "Has mind maps" for new filters. Existing maps still show and can still be edited.
