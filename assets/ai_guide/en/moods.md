Jotsy has two kinds of mood: the **diary mood** that each entry has on its own (10 emojis), and the **mood of the day** you record for a whole day on the calendar (5 levels). They are independent of each other.

## Diary mood
- New diary: after writing, tap + (Publish) at the top right, then tap an emoji under "Mood" in the details panel on the publish page; tap it again to clear. Published diary: open it in the editor and tap the details bar or "More → Edit details" to change it.
- The 10 emojis go from low to happy. The same panel has "Energy" with 5 levels, which you can switch off if you don't want to record it.
- The mood shows on diary cards and in the preview page's details bar. It is also used by the calendar's "Mood" day-cell style and the "Most frequent mood" in the monthly summary, mood filters in the calendar and search, the mood rule of smart notebooks, and the "Mood trend" and "Mood breakdown" cards on the Explore page.
- Diary templates can preset a mood too.

## Customizing mood emojis
Go to Settings → Mood emojis (in the "Organize" group).
- The 10 slots run from low to happy. Replace any of them with another emoji; leave a slot empty to use its default.
- The swatch to the left of each emoji is that mood's color, used by the "Mood" color basis on the reading page and by mood colors on diary cards. Tap the swatch to change it.
- Tap "Save" to apply. "Reset" restores the default emojis and colors (also needs Save).
- Diaries you already wrote keep the emoji you picked back then.
- Mood emojis and mood colors are a preference on this device: they are not included in backups and are not synced over LAN.

## Mood of the day
- On the calendar, **long-press** a day. The top of the panel is "Mood of the day": Awful, Bad, Meh, Good, Great. Tap one to record it. If one is set, tap "Clear" to remove it; you can undo within 4 seconds.
- You can record it even on days without a diary. A day with a mood turns into that mood's color and face in the calendar grid.
- It belongs to the day itself, has nothing to do with the moods of that day's entries, and isn't affected by calendar filters. When the year view is colored by mood, it takes priority.
- The "Mood trend" card on the Explore page has an "Include day moods" option to count it in.
- To hide it, open "Calendar display" in the calendar's top bar and turn off "Mood of the day" under "Sections". Recorded moods are kept.

## Notes
- The mood of the day is set on the calendar, so the Calendar tab must be on (Settings → Features → Calendar).
- The mood of the day is included in full backups and syncs over LAN to paired devices.
- The AI assistant can change a diary's mood and record the mood of the day, for example "Set yesterday's mood to Good".
