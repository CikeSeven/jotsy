Notebooks let you file diaries into separate books. A diary can be in several notebooks at once, and removing it from a notebook never deletes it.

## Where to find it
- The switcher capsule at the front of the home tag bar (it normally says "All entries"): tap it to open the shelf, pick a notebook, and the home page shows only that notebook's entries. The shelf also has "Not in a notebook", listing entries that aren't in any notebook. The app reopens in the notebook you were last in.
- Management page: Settings → Notebooks, or "Manage" at the top right of the shelf.

## Creating a notebook
Tap "＋ New notebook" on the shelf, or "+" at the top right of the management page. You can set the name, description, icon, color and cover (5 layouts, 4 textures, an image of your own or one picked "From diaries"), plus:
- Type: **Regular** (you pick the entries) or **Smart** (collects entries automatically by tags, mood, date, keyword, media, weather, location, cover and pin; up to 12 rules, with "Match all" or "Match any"; you can "Start from an example").
- Parent notebook: put it inside another notebook as a sub-notebook, up to three levels. Opening the parent also shows the sub-notebook's entries. Smart notebooks can't hold sub-notebooks. You can also tap "New" in the parent's header, or use "More → New sub-notebook" on the management page.
- Privacy (regular only): Normal, Hide from All entries, or Lock.
- Writing goal (regular only): a number of entries per day, week or month; progress shows on the header, with no reminders.
- Default order and Template for new entries.

## Adding and removing entries
- While writing, use the "Notebooks" row in the details panel.
- On the preview page: "More → Add to notebook".
- On the home page, long-press to start selecting, then tap "Add to notebook" in the top bar. Inside a regular notebook, "More actions" also has "Remove from this notebook" and "Move to…", both undoable.
- While the home page is inside a regular notebook, the bottom-right button becomes "Write in X", and new entries go into that notebook.
- On a computer, right-click a card and choose "Add to notebook…".

## Inside a notebook
The header card above the list shows entries, words and time span, followed by a few buttons:
- Add entries (regular only): pick existing diaries to add.
- Contents (regular only): create chapters, drag entries and chapters to reorder, and select entries to "Move to chapter…". With "Book order" as the sort, the home list follows the contents, and pinning has no effect.
- Stats: entries, words, mood breakdown, frequent tags, writing rhythm and more, plus "Open a random entry".
- New (sub-notebook): shown while the notebook can hold sub-notebooks but has none yet; once it has some, the header shows a "Sub-notebooks" section for switching and adding more.

A regular notebook with chapters has a row of chapter chips at the bottom of the header (All, each chapter, No chapter); tap one to see only that chapter's entries.

The header's menu at the top right lets you edit, export as a book, close or delete the notebook. Export as book uses the same panel as a normal export, with all seven formats: PDF, Word document, Markdown, Web page and Plain text can turn on "Book structure" (title page, contents and chapter headings, on by default); Spreadsheet (CSV) and Jotsy diary file export as usual, without book structure.

## Privacy and locks
With "Hide from All entries" or "Lock", the notebook's entries stay out of All entries, search, the calendar, Explore and exports, and only show inside that notebook. A locked notebook asks you to verify your identity before opening, editing or deleting (the same method as the app lock, see "App lock and passcode") and locks again after you leave the app. Sub-notebooks of a private parent are private too.

## Managing notebooks
- Reordering: on the management page, hold the drag handle at the end of a row (or long-press the row) and drag. Notebooks can only be reordered among those with the same parent.
- Each notebook's "More" on the management page lets you edit, pin, add a new sub-notebook, move it under another parent, merge it into another (regular only), close it (it moves to "Closed" on the shelf; "Reopen" is in the same place), convert a smart notebook to a regular one, or delete it. When deleting you can choose "Delete notebook only" or "Also move entries to trash", and undo within 4 seconds.
- Long-press a notebook on the shelf to quickly edit, pin, close / reopen or delete it.

## Notes
- Notebooks are on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Features. While off, entries in hidden and locked notebooks stay out of sight; turning notebooks back on restores them.
- Linux has no system verification, so set an app passcode in Settings → App lock & passcode before locking a notebook.
- Search results can be saved with "Save as smart notebook", and a date range on the calendar with "Save as notebook".
- The AI assistant can create and rename notebooks and add or remove entries, for example "Create a Travel notebook and add the entries tagged #travel".
