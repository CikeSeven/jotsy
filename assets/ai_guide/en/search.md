The search page finds diaries by title and text (and by comments when the comments feature is on), and lets you combine tags and filters.

## Where to find it
- Tap the search bar at the top of the Diaries tab. On a computer, press Ctrl + F (⌘ + F on macOS).
- Tapping a tag in the preview page's details bar or on the Explore page opens search with that tag selected.
- When the home page is inside a notebook, the search bar says "Search in “X”" and only searches that notebook. Tap the × on that notebook's condition capsule on the search page to search all diaries instead.

## How to use it
1. Type keywords. Separate several words with spaces to find entries containing all of them (up to 8 words; English letter case doesn't matter).
2. The row of capsules under the search box holds the filters. Open one and pick a value; it applies right away:
   - Tags: pick several; only entries with all selected tags are shown.
   - Date: date range, recent days, this year, a month every year, or this day every year.
   - Mood: one or more moods.
   - Media: has photos, recordings, videos or attachments.
   - Weather, Location: contains some text, e.g. "rain" or "Kyoto".
   - Include archived: archived entries are left out by default; turn this on to include them.
3. The result count shows above the results, with a sort button on the right: Recently updated, Newest or Oldest. Matches in titles and excerpts are highlighted.
4. Tap the × on an active filter capsule to remove it. If nothing is found, tap "Clear filters".

## Recent searches and frequent tags
Before you enter anything, the page shows "Recent searches" (up to 10; tap one to search again, remove single items or clear them all) and "Frequent tags" (tap one to add it as a condition). Recent searches stay on this device and are not backed up.

## Save as smart notebook
Once you've set conditions, tap the magic-wand button "Save as smart notebook" to the right of the search box. It fills in a smart notebook with your current keywords, tags and filters, and matching entries will be collected automatically (the notebook scope and "Include archived" aren't carried over). This needs the notebooks feature; see "Notebooks".

## Notes
- Entries in hidden or locked notebooks don't appear when searching all diaries.
- Sealed time capsules only match by title.
- On a computer, Esc clears the text if there is some, and leaves search if the box is empty.
- The AI assistant can find diaries for you too, for example "Find the entries from last year where I wrote about the beach".
