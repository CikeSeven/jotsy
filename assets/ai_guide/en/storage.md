Storage shows how much space Jotsy takes and what it is made of, and lets you clear cache and delete or compress photos and videos you don't need.

## Where
Settings → Storage (in the "Data & security" group; its subtitle shows how much is used).

## See the usage
- **Device storage** (Android only): the phone's capacity, how much is used, and Jotsy's share.
- **Used by Jotsy**: a ring chart by category: images, videos, recordings, attachments, covers, backgrounds, fonts, diary data, cache, unused files and more, with the total in the middle.
- **Largest files**: the 5 biggest media files; "See all" opens the full list.
- **Media**: images, videos, recordings, attachments, covers and large files; tap one to see its list.
- **Media over time**: media added each month or year, by entry date.
- "Rescan" at the top right measures everything again.

## Free up space
The "Free up space" group:
- **Cache**: tap "Clear"; cache used in the last hour is kept.
- **Unused files**: files no entry uses; tap "Clear".
- **Tidy up diary data**: frees space left behind by deleted content; tap "Tidy up".
- **Entries in the trash**: files used only by entries in the recycle bin; they are freed once you delete those entries for good in the recycle bin.
- **Photos & videos to compress** (Android only): open it to pick what to compress.

"Clean up" under "Used by Jotsy" clears the cache and unused files and tidies up diary data in one go. None of this touches anything in use, so no confirmation is needed.

## Delete and compress
1. Filter the media list at the top (All / Images / Videos / Recordings / Attachments / Covers, plus Large files, Compressible and Trash only). Sort by Largest first, Newest first or Oldest first; images and videos can also be shown as a grid.
2. Tap an item for details, including where it is used; long-press to multi-select.
3. **Delete**: the file is first removed from the entries, notebook covers, comments and drafts that use it, then deleted. The confirmation says what is affected and how much is freed. It can't be undone, and comments left with no text or images are deleted too.
4. **Compress** (Android only): choose High quality, Balanced or Save space for photos and for videos. Compressed files replace the originals and can't be restored; photos keep their capture time and location. Files that are already small, animated images and images with transparency are skipped, and files that don't get noticeably smaller keep their original.

## Notes
- You can't delete or compress while an entry is open for editing or an undo prompt is still showing; close the editor or wait for the prompt to go.
- Media from private entries is hidden until you tap "Show", and media in locked notebooks needs identity verification first. Media in sealed time capsules always stays hidden.
- Deleting and compressing reach your other devices with the next LAN sync. Older cloud backups keep the original files until they are pruned by the Keep setting.
- On computers there is no compression or device storage card, and videos show as icons.
- Only you can clean up, delete and compress; the AI assistant can open the Storage page for you.
