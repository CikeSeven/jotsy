Tags sort your diaries into topics such as "Travel" or "Reading". A diary can carry several tags, and each tag has its own color. Tags appear as small "# name" capsules on cards, the preview page and the filter bar.

## Adding tags to a diary
- New diary: when you finish writing, tap + (Publish) at the top right, then tap the "Tags" row in the details panel on the publish page and tick the tags you want.
- Published diary: open it in the editor, tap the details bar under the title (or "More → Edit details") to open the details panel, then tap "Tags".
- The tag picker has a search box at the top (pinyin full spelling and initials work for Chinese names), and a button on the right that switches between "Default order" and "Alphabetical". If nothing matches, tap "Create “…”" to make a new tag right away.

## Tag bar on the home page
Below the search bar on the Diaries tab is the tag bar (the first capsule switches notebooks, see "Notebooks").
- Tap a tag to show only diaries with that tag; tap it again to clear.
- Long-press a tag (on a computer you can also Ctrl- or ⌘-click) to add it to or remove it from the selection. With several tags selected, only diaries that have all of them are shown.
- The ✕ at the left end clears the filter.
- When the tags don't fit on one line, drag the tag bar down to expand it into several rows; tap "Collapse tags" at the bottom or swipe up to close it.
- To keep your selected tags the next time you open the app, go to Settings → Tag Management and turn on "Tag memory".

## Searching by tag
Tapping a tag in the preview page's details bar, in the Tags card on the Explore page, or under "Frequent tags" on a notebook's stats page opens the search page with that tag selected. You can add keywords and other filters there; see "Searching diaries".

## Setting tags on several diaries at once
On the home page, long-press a diary to start selecting, pick the diaries, then tap "More actions → Set tags" in the top bar. Tags that all selected diaries have show a check, tags only some have show a partial mark. Tap to change, search or create tags as needed, then tap "Done". You can undo within 4 seconds.

## Managing tags
Go to Settings → Tag Management (in the "Organize" group).
- Tap "+" at the top right to create a tag: enter a tag name and choose a color.
- Tap a row to edit it: rename it or change its color. Every diary with that tag updates too.
- Drag the handle at the end of a row to reorder. The home tag bar and the picker's "Default order" follow this order.
- Tap the delete button on a row and confirm to delete a tag. The diaries stay; they just no longer carry that tag. This deletion can't be undone.

## Notes
- How many tags a card shows: Settings → Appearance → Diary card style → Content → Diary card tags (0–20).
- Tag Management has no merge option. With the AI assistant turned on, you can ask it to tidy tags, for example "Merge #running into #exercise" or "Add #business-trip to this week's entries". It can also create, rename and delete tags.
