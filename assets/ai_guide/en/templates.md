Diary templates are ready-made formats you can apply to a blank new entry with one tap. A template can also set tags, mood and a time capsule. There are 8 built-in templates (Daily journal, Morning pages, Evening review, Gratitude journal, Question of the day, Weekly review, Untangle a feeling, Reading notes), and you can make your own.

## Where to find them

- "Start from a template" under the title of a blank new entry;
- "Choose a template" in the top-right menu of the new entry page;
- "Start from a template" in the "Continue a draft" sheet when you have drafts;
- The management page: Settings → Diary templates.

## Starting from a template

1. Start a blank entry. "Start from a template" appears under the title with up to 5 templates: ones suggested for the time of day, weekday and so on come first, marked "Suggested", then recently used ones.
2. Tap one to apply it; you can undo within 4 seconds. Tap All to open the template sheet, where you can search and filter by Suggested, Mine or category. Tap a row to preview it, then "Use this template". Templates with the question of the day offer "Another question".
3. Templates only apply to a blank entry; if you've already typed, you'll see a note instead. If you leave without changing an applied template, no draft is left behind.

## Managing and editing templates

In Settings → Diary templates:
- At the top right are New template and "Import a template code" (imports a template code someone shared, from the clipboard).
- My templates can be dragged to reorder. Built-in templates can be edited directly, and edited ones can be put back with Restore original.
- The menu on each row has Edit, Duplicate, Copy template code and Delete. Deleted built-in templates can be brought back from "Restore deleted built-ins" at the end of the list.
- The template editor has two tabs, Content and Settings. Content holds the title and body; you can use lists, checklists, callouts, toggles, tables and dividers, but not images, videos, recordings or other media. Settings holds the name, short description, category, icon and color; "Also set when applied" presets tags, mood, energy and a time capsule (One week, One month or One year); "When to suggest" chooses the time of day, days of the week and first or last 3 days of the month.
- You can also save an entry as a template: More → Save as template on the preview page, or Save as template in the editor's top-right menu. Images and other media aren't kept.

## Template variables

In the template editor, tap "Insert a variable". Variables are filled in when the template is applied: date, weekday, time, year, month, day, week number, day of the year, year progress, lunar date, solar term and question of the day, written like `{{date}}` or `{{lunar}}`. Time takes a format after a colon, such as `{{time:h:mm a}}`. Date variables follow the entry's date, so a backfilled entry gets that day's values.

## Applying automatically

In Settings → Diary templates → Apply automatically:
- "By time of day": set a start and end time (it can run overnight), days (leave all unselected for every day) and a template. Up to 12 time ranges; they only apply when writing for today.
- "Other times" → Default template: used outside every time range and when writing for another day.
- An automatically applied template can be undone too. Each notebook can also have a "Template for new entries" that applies when you write in that notebook.

## Good to know

- Turn off "Suggest templates for new entries" to hide the suggestion strip.
- Diary templates can be turned off entirely in Settings → Features. The strip and automatic templates disappear, and your templates are kept.
