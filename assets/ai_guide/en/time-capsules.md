A time capsule is an entry written for the future. When you publish it you set an unlock time; until then the text stays sealed, even from you, and it opens for reading only when the time comes.

## How to write one

1. Write a new entry as usual and tap + (Publish) at the top right.
2. In the publish page's details panel, tap Time lock (it shows "Not sealed").
3. In "Choose unlock time", tap One week, One month or One year, or pick a date with the wheel. Switch between "Date only" and "Exact time". The earliest is tomorrow (the next minute with Exact time) and the latest is ten years from now. After confirming, the row shows "Unlocks" plus the date; to undo it, tap "Remove time lock".
4. When you go back, a sealing animation plays and confirms "Locked until …".

Other ways to start:
- On the calendar, long-press a future date and choose "Write a time capsule", or tap "Write a capsule for this day" on an empty future date. The unlock time is preset to that day.
- Diary templates can preset a time capsule (One week, One month or One year). See "Diary templates".

With a capsule set, the entry's publish time follows the unlock time, and the publish time row reads "Follows the time lock".

## While it's sealed

- In the diary list it appears as a sealed card showing only the title, mood and weather, and a countdown such as "Unlocks in 3d". The text isn't shown.
- Opening it shows the "Sleeping moment" page, where you can only "Change unlock time" or Delete. You can't read or edit the text.
- Sealed capsules are never exported or sent to AI, search only matches their title, diary link cards show only the title and unlock date, and they have no comments section.

## When it opens

At the unlock time the capsule becomes a normal entry you can read and edit. The preview page shows a note above the text, such as "When you wrote this diary, you felt … How do you feel today?". To hide it, turn off "Unlocked capsule note" in Settings → Appearance → Diary preview page → Text.

## Where to see them

- The "Time capsules" card on Explore shows how many capsules are waiting, how many days are left, and ones opened recently.
- On the calendar, days with sealed capsules show a lock badge; turn it off in the calendar's "Calendar display" options.
- When the diary list is sorted by publish date, a capsule sits at its unlock date.

## Good to know

- Time capsules are on by default and can be turned off in Settings → Features. Then the time lock no longer appears when publishing, but existing capsules stay sealed and still open on time.
- To read a sealed capsule early, the only way is to move its unlock time earlier on the "Sleeping moment" page.
