To-dos are a task list separate from your diary, with due dates, reminders, repeats and priorities. Checkboxes inside a diary's text are unrelated to them.

## Turning it on
To-dos are off by default: turn on "To-dos" in Settings → Features and a "To-dos" tab appears after "Diaries" in the bottom bar. Turning it off only hides the entries and stops reminders; your to-dos are kept.

## Views and lists
Tap the page title (such as "Today ▾"; wide windows show them in the left column) to switch views: Today, Planned, Important, All, Inbox and Completed, followed by your lists. To-dos not in a list live in the Inbox. Use "New list" to create one with a name, color and icon. The page's "More" menu lets you edit or delete a list, "Manage lists", change the sort, show or hide completed items, and clear completed. Deleting a list deletes its to-dos too (undoable from the notice).
- Sections: inside a list, "Add section" at the end splits the list into parts (such as "Design" and "Shopping"). Tap a section title to fold it; the "+" beside it adds a to-do to that section, and "⋯" renames, moves up or down, or deletes it (its to-dos go back to "No section", or tick the box to delete them too; undo from the notice). With manual sorting, drag a to-do past a section title to move it there; "Section" in the details and "More → Move to section" while selecting do the same. Smart views show "List · Section" on each to-do.
- Sort by: Manual, Due date, Priority or Date created. With Manual, in the Inbox or a list you can hold the handle at the end of an open to-do and drag it to reorder (not while selecting).
- Search: the magnifier "Search to-dos" in the page header looks through titles, notes and steps; completed to-dos are listed too (open ones first) and matches are highlighted. Below the search box you can filter by status, list, tag, high priority or having a date, even without typing a keyword. On a computer, Ctrl / ⌘ + F on the to-do tab opens to-do search.

## Tags
To-dos have their own tags, completely separate from diary tags: diary tag management, the tag bar and search never show to-do tags, and to-dos never show diary tags.
- Tagging: type "@home" while adding a to-do (with a space before it; the @ in an email address doesn't count), or tap "Tags" in the details or the new to-do panel to pick or create one. Each to-do can have up to 10.
- Browsing: the "Tags" group in the view panel and the left column lists every to-do tag; open one to see its open to-dos, and new to-dos added there get the tag. "Open to" can be a tag too.
- In bulk: select to-dos, then "More → Add tag" at the bottom; on a computer you can drag to-dos onto a tag in the left column.
- Managing: "Manage" beside the Tags group renames a tag (using an existing name merges the two) or deletes it (it is only removed from the to-dos; they stay). Both can be undone from the notice.

## Adding to-dos
- Tap "New to-do" at the bottom right, type in "What needs doing?" and press Enter. The panel stays open so you can keep adding; the buttons below set Date, Reminders, Repeat, List and Priority.
- You can also type directly into "Add a to-do" at the end of the list.
- Smart recognition: typing "tomorrow 3pm #Work !!!" picks out the date and time, the list and the priority (! / !! / !!! mean low / medium / high).
- Organize with AI: with the AI assistant on (and "To-do helper" on under Features), a sparkle button sits next to the send button. Write a paragraph (such as "pay rent tomorrow, book the dentist before Friday") and tap it: the AI turns it into several to-dos, each showing its date, list, tags and priority. Untick the ones you don't want, edit titles, tap × to drop a value, then tap "Add N to-dos" to create them all at once.

## Completing, editing and deleting
- Tap the checkbox to complete a to-do; you can undo from the notice. On a phone, swipe right to complete and left to delete.
- Tap a to-do to open its details: edit the title, set date, reminders, repeat, priority, list and linked diary, and add steps and notes. "More" lets you duplicate or delete it.
- AI breakdown: with the AI assistant on, the Steps card in the details has "AI breakdown" at the top right. The AI suggests steps from the title and note without repeating existing ones; untick the ones you don't want and tap "Add N steps" to put them after the existing steps. You can stop it at any time while it writes.
- Long-press to start selecting; the bottom bar can complete, change the date, move to a list, set priority or delete in bulk. The Overdue group has "Move all to today".
- On a computer, right-click a to-do for Mark as done, Reschedule…, Move to list… and Delete.

## Dates, reminders and repeats
- Date: Today, Tomorrow, Next Monday or Pick a date…, then optionally set a time or make it all day.
- Reminders: for timed to-dos, at due time or some minutes, hours or days before; all-day to-dos remind at a set time on the day or the day before; you can also pick a custom time. Each to-do can have up to 5 reminders. Reminders count from the due date: a to-do without a date never reminds you, adding a reminder to one sets its date to today, and removing the date stops its reminders. Notifications have "Done" and "Snooze" buttons.
- Repeat: Daily, Workdays (China public holidays), weekly, monthly, yearly or Custom… (interval, weekdays, end condition; monthly repeats can fall on a day of the month, the Nth weekday such as the second Wednesday, or the last day of the month; "Count from completion" suits things like watering plants). Completing a repeating to-do moves it to the next occurrence.

## With diaries and the calendar
- On the preview page, "More → Add a to-do" creates a to-do linked to that diary. If the diary has a checklist, "Copy checklist to to-dos" also appears (the diary itself is unchanged). Linked to-dos show under "Related to-dos" at the end of the diary.
- On the calendar, days with to-dos get a checkbox badge, the selected day shows "To-dos for this day", and long-pressing a date offers "Add a to-do". Turn this off under Calendar display → Show to-dos.

## Focus timer
- Start: "More → Start focus" at the top right of a to-do's details; on a computer, pick a row with the arrow keys and press F. The focus page shows a large ring and a countdown, with Pause / Resume, "Add 5 minutes" and "End" below.
- When it runs out you can "Take an N-minute break", "Complete this to-do" or start "Another round"; every few rounds there is a long break. Lengths and the long-break interval are under Settings → To-dos → "Focus timer".
- The timer keeps going when you leave the page: a "Focusing · 12:34" pill appears at the top of the To-dos page, tap it to go back. On phones the notification shade shows the countdown and you get an alert at the end; on computers alerts only appear while Jotsy is open.
- A session is kept once it reaches 1 minute; ending early keeps the time actually focused. The details show how long you've focused on a to-do in total, and the Completed view and the Explore to-dos card show this week's focus time. Focus records go with full backups and LAN sync; if you delete a to-do and undo, its total is unchanged.
- If you don't need it, turn off "Focus timer" (under "To-dos") in Settings → Features; existing records are kept.

## Settings and notes
Go to Settings → To-dos (in the "Organize" group) for Show completed, Open to, New to-dos go to, Smart recognition, Notification permission, All-day reminder time, Snooze for and Daily digest.
- Daily digest (off by default): once on, a notification arrives every day at "Digest time" (8:00 by default) saying how many to-dos are due today, how many of them are overdue, and the first few to do; nothing is sent on days with nothing due, and tapping it opens the to-dos' Today view.
- On phones, reminders are delivered by the system on time; the app asks for notification permission the first time you add a reminder. Android also has "Hide content on the lock screen", and "Reminders late or missing?" explains what to do if reminders are often late.
- On computers, reminders only appear while Jotsy is open; reminders that come due while it's closed won't pop up.
- Deleted to-dos don't go to the recycle bin; you can only undo from the notice.
- The AI assistant can create, change, complete, reopen and delete to-dos, set reminders, repeats, steps and to-do tags, and create, rename or delete lists, for example "Remind me to hand in the report tomorrow at 3pm", "Remind me every Monday to send the weekly report" or "Split groceries into milk and eggs". It can also change to-do settings such as showing completed to-dos, sort order, the view to open on, the default list and smart recognition.
