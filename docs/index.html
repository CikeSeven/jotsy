为什么选择 Jotsy

  • 私密：数据全部存在本机，没有账号和服务器；支持应用锁，备份可加密。
  • 好写：富文本编辑，图片、视频、附件、录音随手插入。
  • 有温度：每篇日记都能记下心情、精力、天气和地点。
  • 好回顾：农历日历、年度热力图、那年今日、时间胶囊。
  • 不锁定：ZIP 备份，备份到 WebDAV、对象存储或你自己的 Google Drive / Dropbox 等网盘；单篇可导出长图 / Markdown / PDF；支持从 18 款应用导入。

Why Jotsy

  • Private: everything stays on your device, with no account and no server; app lock and encrypted backups.
  • Easy to write: rich text with photos, videos, attachments and voice recordings.
  • Personal: record mood, energy, weather and place with every entry.
  • Easy to look back: lunar calendar, yearly heatmap, on-this-day and time capsules.
  • No lock-in: ZIP backups to WebDAV, object storage or your own Google Drive / Dropbox; export entries as image, Markdown or PDF; import from 18 apps.
