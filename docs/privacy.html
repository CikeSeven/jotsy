概述

Jotsy 是一款开源的本地日记应用。Jotsy 没有账号体系，也没有开发者运营的服务器；开发者不会收集、接收或存储你的任何日记内容或个人信息。

你的数据保存在哪里

日记、标签、纪念日、图片、视频、录音和附件都保存在你设备上应用的私有目录中，设置保存在设备本机。卸载应用或清除应用数据会删除这些内容。你可以随时导出完整备份（可加密）。如果你使用局域网同步，这些数据还会经加密连接同步到你配对的其他设备上，并保存在那些设备上；同步用的设备身份密钥只保存在本机，不会写入备份。AI 助手生成的内容（读后回应、回顾与对话）同样只保存在本机，随完整备份导出，不参与局域网同步。

局域网同步只在你打开 设置 › 数据与隐私 › 局域网同步 时，或配对之后应用在前台时联网，切到后台即停止，不在后台运行；从没配对过设备时，不打开同步页就不会监听端口或在局域网广播。只有你在两台设备上都确认核对码、或用一次性二维码配对过的设备才能同步，连接全程端到端加密。「可被发现」开启时，Jotsy 会在局域网内广播你设置的设备名，让附近的 Jotsy 找到你；可以在同步页关闭，关闭后不再广播，已配对的设备仍可同步。

网盘备份

网盘备份把你的数据上传到你自己的网盘或服务器上：可以是一个完整备份 ZIP，也可以是增量备份（只上传新增与变化的内容，照片等媒体按内容去重，只传一次）。两种都可以加密。

  • 自动备份：默认关闭。你在某个备份位置开启后，Jotsy 会在打开或离开应用时，按你选的频率（每天或每周）静默做一次增量备份。数据没有变化时不联网；新增内容超过 50 MB 时等你手动备份。只上传到你开启了自动备份的那个位置。
  • 访问范围最小化：Google Drive 只申请 drive.file 权限，Jotsy 只能看到它自己创建的「Jotsy」文件夹及其中的备份，无法读取你网盘里的其他文件；OneDrive、Dropbox 使用应用专属文件夹；百度网盘只能访问「我的应用数据」中的本应用目录；阿里云盘只访问你授权的文件夹。
  • 用途：仅用于上传、列出、下载和删除你的 Jotsy 备份。
  • 凭据只在本机：WebDAV / S3 的地址、密码、访问密钥，以及网盘的授权令牌，只保存在你的设备上，不会发送给开发者，也不会写入备份文件。
  • 随时撤销：在应用中移除备份位置会删除本机保存的凭据；你也可以在 Google 账号、 Microsoft 账号、 Dropbox 等服务的账号设置中撤销 Jotsy 的访问权限。撤销不会删除已上传的备份文件。

Google 用户数据

Jotsy 对从 Google API 获取的信息的使用和传输，遵守 Google API 服务用户数据政策，包括其中的「有限使用」要求。Jotsy 只在你的设备上直接与 Google Drive 通信，用于保存和读取你的备份；这些数据不会被传输给开发者或任何第三方，不会用于广告，不会被人工查看，也不会用于训练任何人工智能或机器学习模型。

AI 助手

AI 助手默认关闭。开启后需要你自己填写 AI 服务的地址与密钥，Jotsy 不提供、也不中转任何 AI 服务。

  • 只在你操作时联网：写作助手、读后回应、写作灵感、回顾与对话都只在你点按后才发送；“发表后自动回应”默认关闭，开启后也只发给你已经确认过的服务。首次发往某个服务（以及按你的设置发送多篇日记）前会说明发往哪里、发送多少内容，由你确认。
  • 直接发出：内容从你的设备直接发给你配置的服务，开发者收不到。服务商会按它自己的隐私政策处理请求。
  • 私密内容：私密（隐藏或上锁）日记本中的日记、回收站里的日记和未解封的时间胶囊不会作为多篇材料发送；对单篇私密日记使用 AI 需要你在 AI 隐私设置里另行允许。你还可以排除指定的日记本和标签，并关闭随正文发送的日期、心情、标签、天气或地点。
  • 凭据只在本机：AI 服务的地址、密钥与用量统计只保存在你的设备上，不会写入备份，也不参与局域网同步。
  • 生成的内容：回应、回顾与对话保存在本机，随完整备份导出；可以在 AI 隐私设置里一键清除。

权限

  • 网络：用于上述联网功能。
  • 麦克风：仅在你录音时使用。
  • 位置（可选）：仅在你开启自动获取地点与天气时使用。
  • 照片、视频与文件：仅在你选择插入或导入时访问你选中的内容。
  • 相册照片与视频（可选）：补写过去某天的日记时，在你点按「允许访问」后列出相册里那天拍的照片和视频，供你预览并挑选插入；只申请图片与视频的读取权限，不读取音频与照片的位置信息，预览只在设备上进行，也不会改动相册中的原文件。
  • 安装应用：仅用于应用内更新，把通过大小、校验值与签名核对的安装包交给系统安装器。
  • 相机（可选，仅 Android）：只在你扫描局域网同步的配对二维码时使用；画面只在设备上识别，不会保存或发送。
  • Wi-Fi 状态与组播（局域网）：仅用于局域网同步时发现同一网络里的 Jotsy 设备。

Overview

Jotsy is an open-source, local-first diary app. Jotsy has no account system and no developer-operated servers. The developer does not collect, receive or store any of your diary content or personal information.

Where your data lives

Entries, tags, anniversaries, photos, videos, recordings and attachments are stored in the app's private directory on your device, and settings stay on the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes them. You can export a full (optionally encrypted) backup at any time. If you use LAN sync, the same data is also synced over an encrypted connection to the other devices you paired, and stored on them; the device identity key used for syncing stays on the device and is never written into backups. Content generated by the AI assistant (reflections, recaps and chats) is also stored only on the device, included in full backups and never synced over LAN.

LAN sync goes online only while Settings › Data & Privacy › LAN sync is open or, once you have paired a device, while the app is in the foreground. Cloud backup

Cloud backup uploads your data to your own drive or server, either as a full backup ZIP or as incremental backups. Incremental backups upload only what is new or changed; photos and other media are deduplicated by content and uploaded once. Both can be encrypted.

  • Automatic backup: off by default. When you turn it on for a backup location, Jotsy quietly makes an incremental backup when you open or leave the app, at the frequency you choose (daily or weekly). Nothing is sent when your data has not changed, and if more than 50 MB is new it waits for you to back up manually. It uploads only to the location where you turned it on.
  • Minimal access: for Google Drive, Jotsy requests only the drive.file scope, so it can see only the "Jotsy" folder and backups it created and cannot read any other file in your Drive. OneDrive and Dropbox use an app-specific folder; Baidu Netdisk is limited to this app's folder under My App Data; Alipan is limited to the folder you authorize.
  • Purpose: solely to upload, list, download and delete your Jotsy backups.
  • Credentials stay on your device: WebDAV / S3 addresses, passwords and access keys, and drive authorization tokens, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer and never written into backups.
  • Revoke at any time: removing a backup location in the app deletes the credentials saved on your device. You can also revoke Jotsy's access in your Google Account, Microsoft account or Dropbox settings. Revoking access does not delete backups already uploaded.

Google user data

Jotsy's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Jotsy talks to Google Drive directly from your device only to store and read your backups. This data is never transferred to the developer or any third party, never used for advertising, never read by humans, and never used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

AI assistant

The AI assistant is off by default. To use it you enter the address and key of an AI service yourself; Jotsy neither provides nor relays any AI service.

  • Online only when you ask: the writing assistant, reflections, writing prompts, recaps and chats send something only after you tap. "Reflect after publishing" is off by default and, when on, only sends to a service you have already confirmed. Before the first request to a service (and, depending on your settings, before sending several entries) Jotsy shows where the content goes and how much is sent, and waits for you to confirm.
  • Sent directly: content goes straight from your device to the service you configured; the developer never receives it. The service handles requests under its own privacy policy.
  • Private content: entries in private (hidden or locked) notebooks, entries in the trash and sealed time capsules are never sent as multi-entry material; using AI on a single private entry requires a separate permission in the AI privacy settings. You can also exclude notebooks and tags, and stop sending the date, mood, tags, weather or place with the text.
  • Credentials stay on your device: AI service addresses, keys and usage statistics are stored only on your device, never written into backups and never synced over LAN.
  • Generated content: reflections, recaps and chats are stored on the device and included in full backups; you can clear them all in the AI privacy settings.

Permissions

  • Network: for the online features above.
  • Microphone: only while you record audio.
  • Location (optional): only when automatic location and weather is turned on.
  • Photos, videos and files: only the items you pick to insert or import.
  • Photo library (optional): when you write an entry for a past day, Jotsy lists the photos and videos you took that day for you to preview and add, only after you tap "Allow access". It asks for image and video access only, never reads audio or photo locations, previews them on the device only, and never changes the originals.
  • Install apps: only for in-app updates, to hand a package that passed the size, checksum and signature checks to the system installer.
  • Camera (optional, Android only): only while you scan a LAN sync pairing QR code. Frames are processed on the device and never stored or sent.
  • Wi-Fi state and multicast (local network): only for LAN sync, to find Jotsy devices on the same network.

Third-party services

When you use the online features above, the services involved (Amap, QWeather, GitHub and its mirrors, the drives and clouds you use, and the AI service you configure) handle the requests under their own privacy policies. Jotsy contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.

Children

Jotsy does not collect information from children, or from anyone else.
