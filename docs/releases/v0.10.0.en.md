# Release v0.10.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.10.0.md)

## Highlights
- LAN sync: pair your phone and computer on the same Wi-Fi and they merge all your diaries and data both ways — no server, no account. When both sides edited the same diary, both versions are kept.
- Many more text formats: a heading panel (H1–H6), alignment, font size, superscript / subscript, callouts, toggles and tables. Pasting Markdown text turns it into the matching formatting.
- Diary links: type `@` to link to another diary or notebook, or insert a diary card that shows live content. A diary that's been mentioned lists the diaries that mention it.
- Mood palette: each of the 10 moods has its own color, and you can change any of them. The preview page's color basis adds color intensity, paper tint and more.
- A Changelog page in About, readable offline.

## New
### LAN sync
- Settings → Data & Privacy → LAN sync. Available on Android, Windows and Linux; not on iOS yet.
- Pairing: find the other device under Nearby devices and tap Pair. Both devices show the same pairing code, and pairing completes once both confirm. Or show a QR code on one device and scan it with "Scan QR code" on an Android phone (codes last 2 minutes and expire once scanned). If this device has app lock or a locked notebook, you're asked to verify your identity before confirming.
- What syncs: diaries (including those in the recycle bin), tags, notebooks (with their entries, book order and parent notebooks), comments, templates (including edited built-ins), anniversaries, moods of the day, and media such as images and videos. Tag order and whether Notebooks, Anniversaries, Diary templates, Time capsules and Comments are turned on always sync — but features only sync on: turning one off on the other device won't turn it off here. Other settings sync only when both devices turn on "Sync settings".
- What doesn't sync: drafts, imported font files, mood emojis and mood colors, cloud backup sign-ins, the app lock switch, and per-device preferences.
- How merging works: each diary's text, tags, notebooks, archive, recycle bin and pin state are compared separately, and only edits to the same part on both sides count as a conflict. A text conflict saves a copy, so what you wrote on both devices is kept. Deletions only carry over to diaries the other device hasn't changed since. Deleting many diaries at once asks first and keeps them by default; when the two sides don't line up on a first sync or after restoring a backup, you choose which version wins, and the default keeps both.
- Auto sync: when on, Jotsy syncs with paired devices automatically while it's open. If something needs your confirmation, it stops without writing anything and waits for you on the sync page.
- The sync page shows progress and a summary when done, and each device's page keeps a sync history. Discovery and connections work with a VPN or proxy on; if you still can't find the other device, check "Can't find a device?" or enter its address directly.
- Privacy: data goes directly between the two devices, encrypted end to end. With no paired devices, Jotsy only uses the network while the sync page is open; once paired, only while the app is open, stopping as soon as it goes to the background. Turn off "Discoverable" to stay hidden from nearby devices.

### Text formats
- Headings: the heading tool is now a panel where you pick Body or H1–H6 directly. The `/` menu adds H4–H6, and imports keep the original heading levels.
- Alignment (new tool): left, center, right and justified. Justified paragraphs keep their first-line indent.
- Font size (new tool): Small, Body, Large and Extra large; text you type next keeps the current size.
- Superscript / subscript: the cursor, selection and input methods work as usual, and line height doesn't change. On desktop, Ctrl / ⌘ + `.` and Ctrl / ⌘ + `,`.
- Callouts: a colored block with an emoji icon and 6 background tones. With the cursor in a callout, use the toolbar panel to change the icon or tone, or turn it back into body text.
- Toggles: a heading line with content that folds away beneath it. Tap the marker in the editor to collapse or expand; opening one on the preview page only changes the view and never edits the diary.
- Tables: full width with equal columns, never scrolling sideways at any width, so they don't fight the preview page's swipe between diaries. Insert or delete rows and columns, toggle a header row; up to 6 columns in the editor. Tab and the arrow keys move between cells.
- Paste Markdown: text that's clearly Markdown (headings, lists, quotes, code, tables and so on) is converted when pasted. Pasting into a code block or table cell keeps the raw text, and Ctrl / ⌘ + Shift + V on desktop always pastes plain text.
- The new tools sit in the second half of the toolbar and go into More when a row is full; callouts, toggles and tables can also be inserted from the `/` menu. If you've customized the toolbar order, the new tools are added at the end — rearrange them in Settings → Editor → Toolbar order.
- Exports: PDF, Word, HTML, Markdown and plain text all write out callouts, toggles, tables and superscript / subscript. Word and HTML also gain font sizes, alignment and H4–H6.
- Imports and templates: HTML and Markdown imports from other apps recognize superscript / subscript, collapsible sections and well-formed tables, and diary templates can use callouts, toggles and tables.

### Diary links
- Type `@` in the text, then part of a diary title or notebook name (notebook names match pinyin too), and pick a result to insert a link. Or use Link in the toolbar (Ctrl / ⌘ + K on desktop) and choose from the Diaries and Notebooks tabs.
- Diary cards / notebook cards: insert them from the `/` menu. A card shows the diary's live title, date, excerpt and image, or a notebook's cover and entry count. Tap a card while editing to open it, convert it to an inline link or delete it.
- Opening links: tap a link on the preview page to open it; while editing, double-tap or Ctrl / ⌘ + click. Targets in a locked notebook ask you to verify first. Cards for private diaries only say "Private diary", and sealed time capsules only show their title and opening date.
- Backlinks: the end of the preview page lists the diaries that mention this one, and a notebook that's been mentioned shows "Mentioned in N diaries" in its header — tap to see them all.
- Exports and shares keep only the link text, and cards export as a line with their title.

### Mood palette
- Each of the 10 moods has its own color, from indigo, blue, teal and green warming up to orange, coral and rose. With the preview page's color basis or the diary card color source set to Mood, every mood now looks different; before, the happier moods all looked like the app theme.
- Settings → Editor → Mood emojis: each mood gets a color swatch on its left; tap it to change the color. Reset restores both the emojis and the colors.
- Preview page style → Page → Colors adds Color intensity (Soft, True color, Vivid — Soft is the default and matches before), Tint the paper, and When there's no color (App theme or Neutral gray). The settings preview can switch the sample diary's mood so you can see the effect. The Mood color preset now colors by mood and tints the paper.
- The calendar year view and Explore's mood charts keep their colors.

### Changelog
- Settings → About → Changelog lists every release from newest to oldest. Each card shows the highlights; tap Show all for the full notes. The notes ship with the app, so they work offline.

### More
- Tags are tappable: tags in the preview page's info bar, under Top tags on a notebook's stats page, and in Explore's Recap card open a search for that tag. From a notebook's stats page, the search stays within that notebook and its sub-notebooks.
- Sub-notebooks in a notebook's header are now a grid (two columns on phones, more in wide windows). With many sub-notebooks only two rows show, and the last tile reads "N more" — tap to expand them all. New sub-notebook moves to the title row.
- Notebook management: notebooks with sub-notebooks can be collapsed or expanded, and the top bar can collapse them all at once. Dragging a parent moves its sub-notebooks with it, and you can long-press anywhere on a row to drag it.

## Improvements
- The icon tiles in Explore's Weather card fill the whole row instead of leaving a gap on the right.
- Underlines and strikethroughs follow the text color; before, colored text still got lines in the default ink.

## Fixes
- After hiding the keyboard in the editor, tapping where the cursor was didn't bring the keyboard back.
- After editing a long diary and scrolling back up to tap the title, the body kept the focus and the page jumped back down.
- On desktop, selecting text popped up a phone-style copy menu over the text right away; it now appears on right-click and closes with Esc.
- With glass material on, the preview page's top bar was still solid.
- With a first-line indent, underlines, strikethroughs and highlights at the start of a paragraph were drawn into the indent.
- Gray notebook colors showed up as the teal theme color; the gray in template colors did the same.
- "Random diary" on a notebook's stats page never landed on diaries in its sub-notebooks.
- Typing `> ` at the start of a line didn't make a quote, and justified alignment didn't show in the editor.

## Notes
- This version doesn't upgrade the database; diaries, backup files and your settings stay compatible.
- LAN sync needs both devices on this version. Android adds network state, Wi-Fi multicast and camera permissions; the camera is only used to scan pairing codes, and devices without one can still install the app. The privacy policy has been updated to match.
- Pairings and sync state stay on the device and aren't included in backups. After restoring a backup over your data, the first sync with each paired device asks which version wins (keeping both by default).
- Please don't downgrade after upgrading: older versions break tables into separate lines of text and move folded content out of toggles. No text is lost, but editing and saving there changes the structure.
- Mood colors, like mood emojis, are a per-device preference and aren't backed up.
- On Android, install over the existing app to upgrade, or update in the app from Settings → About → Check for updates.
