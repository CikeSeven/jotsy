# Release v0.11.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.11.0.md)

## Highlights
- AI assistant (off by default): connect your own AI service for a writing assistant, reflections, writing prompts, recaps and chatting with your diary. Jotsy only goes online when you tap, and entries in private notebooks are never sent.
- Incremental cloud backups: each backup uploads only what changed, photos are stored once on the drive, and restoring downloads only what this device is missing. Automatic backups can be turned on per location.
- Take photos and record videos right while writing (Android). When writing for a past day, "Photos from this day" now lists that day's videos too, and you can preview them.
- A new text selection menu: long-press selections and desktop right-clicks use a glass menu that matches the app, with Share and other apps' text actions (such as Translate).
- Multi-select on the home page adds Select all and setting tags in bulk.

## New
### AI assistant
- Turning it on: switch on "AI assistant" in Settings → Features, then add your service in Settings → AI assistant → Providers, fetch its model list and check the models you want. Entry points appear once at least one provider and model are set up.
- Supports OpenAI-compatible, OpenAI Responses, Anthropic and Gemini APIs. Presets include DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (Qwen), Kimi, Zhipu, Volcano Ark (Doubao), SiliconFlow, Tencent Hunyuan, MiniMax, OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, xAI and Mistral, and you can also connect Ollama or LM Studio on your computer, or any custom address. Jotsy doesn't provide an AI service; costs follow your provider's billing.
- Writing assistant: open it from the toolbar, the `/` menu or the menu for selected text. Continue, expand, shorten, polish, fix errors, summarize, turn into bullet points, extract to-dos, translate, write as a poem, or make the tone more casual, formal, warm, literary or funny — or write your own commands. Replace the original or insert the result below it; one undo takes it back. Generation keeps going when you hide the keyboard.
- Smart fill: from the editor's ⋮ menu or next to the title, let AI write a title or suggest tags and a mood.
- Reflections: on the preview page, More → AI reflection lets a persona read the entry and respond to you. There are 10 built-in personas, such as Diary assistant, Warm listener, Close friend, Growth coach, Poet and Future self, and you can write your own. Reflections appear after the comments; regenerate them, save one as a comment, or tap Talk more to keep chatting. "Respond after publishing" is off by default.
- Writing prompts: on a new blank entry, tap "Need a writing prompt?" below the title, then tap a question to add it to your entry.
- Recaps: write a recap for this week, last week, this month, last month, this year or custom dates, optionally for a single notebook, choosing which sections to include (mood trends, key moments, growth & change and more) and the voice. Entries cited in a recap open with a tap, and a recap can be saved as an entry or shared as text. Explore adds an "AI insights" card.
- Chat with your diary: the AI icon in the Diaries tab's top bar opens the AI assistant home, where you can ask anything or tap a quick question. Answers cite the related entries, which open with a tap; switch the scope, persona and model at any time, and search, pin or rename past chats.
- Image understanding (on by default): sends the photos in an entry along with the text. Choose how many to send at most, from 1 to 20 (3 by default); if the model can't take images, only the text is sent.
- Privacy: every request starts with a tap from you. Before sending, Jotsy shows which service and model it goes to and how many entries, words and images are included. Private notebooks, the recycle bin and sealed time capsules are never sent, and you can leave out more notebooks and tags. Using AI on a single private entry has to be allowed separately.
- How much is sent: "Send at most" in AI privacy defaults to 100K words and goes from 10K to 1M. Long entries are sent in full, and text is only cut when a request's total goes over the limit.
- Provider settings, API keys and usage stay on this device: they aren't backed up or synced. Chats, reflections and recaps are included in full backups but never synced over the local network. Settings → AI assistant → Usage shows the requests and tokens sent from this device.

### Incremental and automatic cloud backups
- The Back up button on a backup location now makes incremental backups: photos and other media are de-duplicated by content and stored only once on the drive, later backups upload only what's new, and nothing is uploaded when nothing changed. This suits drives with traffic or request limits (such as Jianguoyun).
- When restoring, photos already on the device are reused and only the missing parts are downloaded; restoring on the same device usually takes just a few MB. Merge / overwrite, the warning before overwriting and rollback on failure work as before.
- The password is set on the first incremental backup (leave it empty for no encryption) and used for every backup after that. You'll need it to continue backing up or to restore on another device.
- If the network drops or the app is closed midway, the next backup picks up where it left off; rate limits and network errors are retried automatically.
- Keep: 10, 30, 100 or all, 30 by default. When an old backup is deleted, photos used only by it are cleaned up too.
- Automatic backup: turned on per location, off by default, and available after one successful manual backup. Jotsy checks when you open or leave the app and every hour while you use it, and backs up quietly in the background once a daily or weekly backup is due. If more than 50 MB is new, it waits for you to back up manually. Results show in the location list and on the location's page.
- "Upload full ZIP" is still there; existing ZIP backups are listed and restored as before, and both kinds appear together in the list by time.

### Photos and videos from the camera
- While writing, Insert image now offers Take photo and Insert video offers Record video; the `/` menu adds them too. What you shoot goes straight in at the cursor.
- Photos and videos taken this way are only stored in your diary, not in the system gallery. Android only; the camera permission is requested the first time.

### Photos from this day: videos and preview
- When writing for a past day, "Photos from this day" also lists the videos you took that day, with their length on the thumbnail. Inserted photos and videos are ordered by when they were taken.
- Tap a thumbnail to open the preview: swipe between them, zoom into photos and play videos. Tap the circle in the top-right corner to select, and insert straight from the preview.
- If you upgrade from an older version and only allowed access to photos, you'll see photos first; videos appear after you tap Allow access.

### Text selection menu
- On phones: selecting text brings up a glass menu next to the selection with Cut, Copy, Paste, Select all (and AI when the AI assistant is on). More holds Share for the selected text and, on Android, text actions from other apps (such as Translate). In the diary editor, the result from another app replaces the selection, and one undo restores the original.
- On desktop: right-click opens a vertical menu with keyboard shortcuts shown, and you can use the arrow keys, Enter and Esc.
- The diary text and every text field in the app now use this menu; password fields have no Copy or Share.

### Home page multi-select
- Select all: while selecting, tap "N selected" in the top bar to select every entry in the current list, including ones not loaded yet; the current notebook, chapter and tag filter still apply. Tap again to deselect all while staying in multi-select.
- Set tags: in multi-select, More → Set tags adds or removes tags on all selected entries at once. Tags every selected entry has are checked, and tags only some have are partly checked. You can search or create a tag right there, and undo within 4 seconds afterwards.

### More
- Background & Material: a page with its own background can follow the image of the global background or another page, and its parameters (focus, blur and veils) can follow together; when the source changes its image, this page changes with it.
- Preview page style: the Title tab adds "Line below title" and "Space before text", and the color basis adds "Tint the background veil", so the veil over the background image takes on the entry's color.
- A new Divider tool in the toolbar inserts a divider at the cursor. If you've customized the toolbar order, it's added at the end.
- Diary templates: the time variable can take a format after a colon, such as `{{time:HH:mm}}` or `{{time:h:mm a}}`, and the variables panel adds "Time (12-hour)".

## Improvements
- The toolbar's Indent on body paragraphs now indents only the first line (by two characters), leaving wrapped lines at the margin instead of pushing the whole paragraph right. Lists, to-dos and toggles still indent by level.
- The bars of adjacent quote lines join into one instead of breaking between lines.
- Pasting Markdown is more reliable: text copied from VS Code, notes or chat apps (where the clipboard carries HTML that only wraps the plain text), multi-line text pasted from the keyboard's clipboard panel, and links with a space like `[text] (https://…)` are now converted too. Formatted content from web pages still pastes with its original formatting.
- Pin, archive and delete in home page multi-select now write in one go, so selecting thousands of entries doesn't stall the app.
- HEIC photos from the phone's gallery are converted to JPEG when inserted into an entry or set as a cover or background (Android 9 and later), so the desktop version can show them. HEIC images inserted before are converted automatically after startup. On a computer, an image that hasn't been converted yet explains why and can be opened in another app.

## Fixes
- Long-pressing an empty paragraph on a phone showed no menu, so there was no way to paste.
- With a table at the end of an entry, or followed by an image or a divider, the cursor couldn't leave the table. There's now always a paragraph to write in after a table, and pressing Enter on an empty line in the last row also leaves it.
- After switching between light and dark, or changing the preview page's paper or color basis while a page was open, the text kept its old color — dark text on dark paper or light text on light paper.
- The template editor froze when expanding the toolbar's More tools.
- Pressing Backspace at the start of the line after a divider, image or video pushed it below the text. Backspace now deletes it and the line stays where it is (an empty line is removed first). On desktop, Delete at the end of a line also removes a divider or image right after it.
- When an entry ended with an image, video or table, the cursor couldn't get past it, and images and videos couldn't be deleted. Tapping the space below them now adds a line at the end so you can keep writing.
- On the Windows installed version, the system firewall blocked LAN sync, so devices couldn't be found or connected.

## Notes
- This version upgrades the database (v16 → v17), only adding tables for the AI assistant; existing diaries aren't changed.
- With the AI assistant off, Jotsy makes no AI requests and looks the same as before. Once on, diary content is sent to the service you set up, so check that provider's privacy policy.
- Android adds permission to read videos in the gallery. It belongs to the same "Photos and videos" permission as photos and is only requested when you tap Allow access; the camera permission is now also used to take photos and record videos. The privacy policy has been updated to match (AI assistant, automatic backups and gallery videos).
- Incremental backup files sit in the same backup folder as ZIPs. Older versions can't see them and can't restore incremental backups. The incremental backup password is only stored on this device, so keep it somewhere safe — you'll need it to restore on another device.
- JPEGs converted from HEIC are usually a little larger than the originals, and the original HEIC files are cleaned up after 24 hours. The conversion counts as an edit to the entry and syncs to your other devices over LAN sync.
- The Windows installer adds firewall rules for LAN sync (private networks only) and removes them on uninstall. The portable version doesn't touch the firewall; if devices can't be found, allow Jotsy on private networks in Windows Firewall.
- On Android, install over the existing app to upgrade, or update in the app from Settings → About → Check for updates.
