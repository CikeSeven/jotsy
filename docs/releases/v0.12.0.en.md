# Release v0.12.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.12.0.md)

## Highlights
- App passcode: unlock the app and locked notebooks with a 6-digit PIN, a 4-digit PIN or a pattern instead of your phone's screen lock. App lock now works on Linux too.
- Japanese and Russian interface languages.
- Comments can carry photos, up to 9 each, laid out as an album collage on the preview page.
- Images in an entry can be shown as thumbnails, and your body font can be used for the whole app and for diary cards.
- Pick a place from "Recent places" with one tap while writing. On phones, long images can be saved straight to the gallery, and they no longer include the preview page's top bar.

## New
### App passcode
- Where: Settings → App lock & passcode, then choose "System verification" or "App passcode" under Verification method. The passcode can be a 6-digit PIN, a 4-digit PIN or a pattern.
- PINs are entered on the app's own number pad, without the system keyboard, and are checked as soon as the last digit is in; long-press the delete key to clear. On a computer you can type the digits directly. Patterns connect at least 4 dots in one stroke and are checked when you lift your finger.
- From the 5th wrong try on, you have to wait: 30 seconds, then 1 minute, 5 minutes and 15 minutes.
- On phones you can turn on "Fingerprint / face quick unlock". Turning off app lock, switching the method and changing the passcode still need the passcode.
- App lock, locked notebooks (opening, editing, merging, deleting and re-locking when you come back to the app) and LAN pairing confirmation all use the same method.
- Linux has no system verification; once you set an app passcode, you can turn on app lock and lock notebooks there.

### Japanese and Russian
- Settings → Language adds 日本語 and Русский, and the first launch picks one automatically from the system language.
- Dates follow local conventions, such as `3月12日` in Japanese and `12 мар.` in Russian.
- When the AI assistant's answer language is set to follow the app language, it answers in Japanese or Russian. The built-in Translate translates between the interface language and English (Chinese and English interfaces still translate between Chinese and English).
- Template variables can be written in Japanese or Russian, such as `{{日付}}` or `{{дата}}`, and templates shared between languages still work. Weather descriptions follow the interface language too.

### Photos in comments
- When writing a comment, tap "Add photos" in the bottom-left corner to attach up to 9 photos; a comment can also be just photos.
- On the preview page, photos are laid out as an album collage: one photo keeps its shape, two sit side by side, three show one large and two small, nine form a grid, and so on. Tap any photo to open the viewer and swipe through them.
- You can add or remove photos when editing a comment, and undoing a deleted comment brings its photos back. In Explore, "Recent comments" shows a thumbnail at the end of comments that have photos.
- Full backups, restores, merge imports and LAN sync include comment photos, and exporting to web page, Markdown, Word or PDF writes them out too.

### Image thumbnails in entries
- While editing, tap an image in the text → "Show as thumbnail" to shrink it into a small square (long images show only their top), placed left, center or right following the image's alignment.
- Tap the thumbnail again and choose "Show at normal size" to bring back its earlier size, including any size you set before. Switching to a thumbnail can be undone in one step.
- While reading, tap a thumbnail to open the full image in the viewer.

### Body font for the whole app
- The font chosen in Settings → Editor → Body font & layout is now also used for the titles and excerpts on diary cards (on Home, in search, the archive and the calendar, and in the drafts and recycle bin lists). Dates, tags and other interface text on the cards stay the same.
- A new switch, "Use for the whole app" (off by default), puts menus, settings, dialogs and all other text in that font, keeping sizes and weights unchanged.
- With an imported font, a cold start waits for the font to load before showing Home (up to 1.5 seconds), so the built-in font doesn't flash first.

### Recent places
- While writing, the place field in "Here and now" has a new clock button that opens "Recent places": places you located or typed before, with how many entries used each and when it was last used. Search by name or pinyin, and tap a row to fill it in.
- When the panel is expanded and the place is still empty, your recent places appear right below the field, ready to pick with one tap.
- A place that was located automatically comes back with its coordinates, so you can still switch the address level and get the weather for it.
- The list is gathered from your published entries and isn't stored separately; places from the recycle bin and private notebooks don't appear.

### Save long images to the gallery
- On phones, the share panel adds "Save long image to gallery" below "Share as long image". It makes the same long image and saves it straight to the gallery, without the system share sheet. Photo permission is requested the first time.

### More
- Double-tap the text on the preview page to start editing with the cursor where you tapped. Double-tapping an image, a list bullet, a to-do checkbox and the like doesn't open the editor. Phones only, on by default; turn it off in Settings → Appearance → Diary preview page → Text.
- The app opens in the notebook (or chapter, or Not in a notebook) you were in when you last closed it. If that notebook was deleted or notebooks are turned off, it starts from all entries; a locked notebook asks you to verify first.
- If some tags on Home are hidden beyond the first row of the tag bar and you've never expanded it, a one-time hint shows that you can pull it down; tapping the hint expands it too. It stops after you expand the bar once or after it has appeared 3 times.
- AI assistant: persona prompts, writing commands, About me and Writing style can now be up to 100K characters each, and you can create up to 50 personas and 50 custom writing commands.
- A new "black & white" theme color at the end of the list: buttons, switches and other accents are pure black in light mode and pure white in dark mode, and everything else is gray.

## Improvements
- The Settings home page is now a grouped list: General, Writing, Organize, Data & security, then AI assistant and About. The "Data & Privacy" page is gone: Data Management, LAN sync and App lock & passcode open right from the Settings home page, and cloud backup is at Data Management → Cloud backup. Appearance is split into Theme, Interface and Page styles, and Diary templates and Mood emojis moved to the Settings home page.
- PDF export keeps center and right alignment and first-line indents; Chinese text breaks between characters, so an indent no longer pushes a whole paragraph to the next line; headings are bold with the same size steps as in the app; wrapped list lines align with the text; to-dos are drawn as boxes, with done items grayed and struck through; and dividers are drawn as lines.
- PDF, Word and web page exports keep the size, thumbnail setting and alignment you gave images in the app, and small images are no longer blown up to the full page width.
- Web page export keeps first-line indents and indents nested paragraphs and lists by level.
- Android: the back gesture no longer shows a preview. Swiping back doesn't shrink the current page to reveal the previous page or the home screen; like the back button, it goes back when you let go.

## Fixes
- Choosing any swatch in the gray theme color family left the app in the default teal, and the lightest brown swatch came out yellow-green. The gray family is now truly gray, and delete and other destructive buttons are still red.
- Long images for sharing had the preview page's top-bar buttons and status bar space at the top, and with the top bar set to always show, it repeated in the middle of the image and covered text. Long images now leave out the top bar and the previous / next cards, and the text starts at the top of the image.
- After adding a link at the cursor with Link and then typing, only the first character became part of the link. Everything you type now goes into the link until you type a space or press Enter; this works with pinyin input too.
- With a first-line indent, the background color on the first character of a paragraph was taller than on the rest of the line, and highlighted superscript and subscript text was darker and a different height than the text around it.
- When the cursor landed on a long image taller than the visible area (for example with the keyboard up), the editor kept scrolling back and forth and flickering.

## Notes
- This version upgrades the database (v17 → v18), only adding a column for comment photos; existing diaries aren't changed.
- LAN sync's data format changes along with comment photos: both devices need this version to keep syncing. If the versions differ, sync stops with "Incompatible versions" and nothing is written.
- Restoring a backup from this version in an older version rejects the whole backup if it contains a comment with photos and no text.
- The app passcode is stored only on this device. It isn't backed up or synced and can't be recovered if forgotten; the only way back in is to clear the app's data or reinstall, then restore from a backup.
- The Japanese and Russian translations are new, so corrections are welcome in the issues. The changelog and release notes are only in Chinese and English, and the Japanese and Russian interfaces show the English version.
- PDFs with Chinese text are now laid out character by character and come out about a third larger than before.
- On Android, install over the existing app to upgrade, or update in the app from Settings → About → Check for updates.
