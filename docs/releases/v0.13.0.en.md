# Release v0.13.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.13.0.md)

## Highlights
- New standalone to-dos: lists, reminder notifications, repeats and steps, connected to the calendar and your diaries. Off by default; turn it on in Settings → Features.
- The AI assistant can now act: it searches diaries, runs stats and opens pages, and it can create and edit diaries, organize tags and notebooks, manage to-dos and change common settings, with every change undoable. It also comes with a built-in guide, so you can ask it about any feature of the app.
- A redesigned AI chat: pick a thinking level, find past chats in a side drawer, read answers in sections and export chats.
- New Storage page: see where your space goes, clean up in one tap, and delete or compress large photos and videos.
- Editing an existing diary now uses the same layout as the preview page. Thumbnails sit three to a row, and the cursor can rest next to images.
- A redesigned search with multi-word queries and filters. App lock can lock again after you leave the app, when the screen locks or when idle, and Android can block screenshots.

## New
### To-dos
- Where: Settings → Features → To-dos (off by default). Once on, the home screen gets a To-dos tab after Diaries.
- Views are Today, Planned, Important, All, Inbox and Completed, plus lists you create (color, icon, drag to reorder). Tap "Today ▾" in the header to switch views; lists show their progress. Today has a progress ring with remaining, overdue and completed counts, and overdue to-dos can all be moved to today in one tap.
- Type at the end of the list to add a to-do. Smart recognition picks the date, time, list and priority out of text like "tomorrow 3pm meeting #work !!!"; tap a highlighted part to drop it, or turn recognition off in to-do settings.
- Each to-do can have steps, a date and time, several reminders, a repeat (daily, on weekdays you pick, monthly, yearly, statutory workdays, or custom), a priority, a list, linked diaries and notes. Details open in a sheet you can pull up on phones, and in a side panel on wide windows.
- On phones, swipe right to complete and left to delete. Long-press to multi-select (Ctrl / ⌘ + click on a computer), then complete, reschedule, move, set priority or delete in bulk. Checking off and deleting can be undone.
- Reminders arrive as system notifications with "Done" and "Snooze" buttons; tap one to open that to-do.
- Days with to-dos get a badge in the calendar, "To-dos for this day" lists them, and long-pressing a date lets you add one.
- The diary preview's "More" adds "Add a to-do" and "Copy checklist to to-dos" (copies the entry's checklist into standalone to-dos without changing the entry), and "Related to-dos" appear after the body. Checklists inside entries work as before.
- Full backups, restore, merge import and LAN sync all include to-dos.

### AI assistant: looks up, makes changes, shows the way
- Under Settings → AI assistant → Chat & actions (formerly Chat), turn on "Let the AI use tools" (on by default). The model has to support tool calling ("Supports tool calling" in model settings); if it doesn't, answers fall back to searching your diaries as before.
- Look up: find diaries by keyword, date, tag, mood or notebook, read them in full, count entries, words and streaks with your common moods and tags, and check a day's lunar date, holidays, mood, diaries, to-dos and anniversaries. Private diaries are never read in full, and notebooks and tags excluded in AI privacy never show up.
- Show the way: answers include "Open" cards that name the exact diary or notebook. When you say "open", it opens once the answer finishes.
- Make changes: create, append to and edit diaries; tag, pin, archive, move to trash and comment; create, rename, merge and delete tags; create and rename notebooks and add or remove diaries; manage to-dos (including reminders, repeats, steps and lists), anniversaries and the day's mood; switch theme, language, text size and features, plus 26 common settings for the editor, preview page, calendar and to-dos.
- Every change has "Undo", and "Undo all" appears when one answer made several. Deletions are confirmed in the chat first. Before undoing a diary edit, it checks whether the entry changed since, and asks you if it did.
- New settings: "Let the AI make changes" (on by default), "Confirm every change" (off by default) and "Max steps per reply" (10 / 20 / 40).
- Off limits: private diaries, private or locked notebooks, diaries in the trash and sealed time capsules; nothing is changed while an editor is open. Permanent deletion, backup and restore, import and export, sharing, cloud drives, LAN pairing, app lock and passcode, provider keys, storage cleanup and the AI assistant's own settings only get an "Open" button for you to do yourself.
- Built-in guide: 35 articles cover every feature. For "how do I", "where is" and "can it" questions, the AI reads the guide before answering and offers a button to the right page. Japanese and Russian interfaces read the English guide and answer in the interface language.

### AI chat
- The AI entry opens a new chat directly; the old AI home page is gone. The welcome screen has quick questions, recaps and recent chats. If only the recap module is on, the entry opens recaps.
- Past chats live in a left drawer on phones and a fixed side panel on wide screens: search, grouped by date, rename, pin, export and delete, and tap one to switch to it in place.
- Three chips above the input: scope, persona and "model · thinking". Thinking can be Auto, Off, Low, Medium or High. Each chat remembers its own choice and new chats start with the last one; providers that don't support it fall back automatically.
- Answers come in sections: thinking and lookups fold into "Thought for N seconds · Used N tools", while Open, change and confirmation cards and the answer itself stay visible. Step details read as "conditions / result" instead of JSON.
- Answers, thinking and your own questions can be selected with a long press.
- After a card takes you to a notebook or another home tab, going back returns to the chat at the spot where you left it, and the home screen shows a "Back to chat" bar.
- Export chat: Markdown, plain text or JSON, with or without thinking and steps, for all messages or just the ones you check; then copy, share or save to a file.
- "Save as diary" now opens a new entry with the content, and going back without changes leaves no draft.
- Usage under an answer shows cache hits, like "Input N (cached M) · Output K".

### Storage
- Where: Settings → Data & security → Storage.
- The overview shows the total, a color-coded bar by category and, on Android, the device's free space. "Clean up" removes caches and files nothing uses anymore. Suggestions below point to space you can free: caches, unused files, trash, compressible photos and videos, and tidying the diary database.
- The media page filters by category, large files, compressible and trash-only, sorts by size or date, and shows a list or a grid. Long-press to multi-select and compress or delete in bulk. Tap an item to see it large, along with the diaries, covers, comments and drafts that use it.
- Deleting tells you what's affected first and can't be undone. Compressing starts with a level (photos: High quality / Balanced / Save space; videos: 1080p / 720p / 480p) and an estimated size, and can be canceled. If the result isn't clearly smaller, the original is kept; photos keep their capture time and location.
- Media from private diaries is blurred by default, and showing it asks you to verify if the notebook is locked. Sealed time capsules always stay blurred.
- Compression is available on Android only.

### Reading-style editing
- Editing an existing diary now uses the preview page's layout: theme and paper color, header image, title style, info bar, margins and text width all match the preview, and you edit the title and body in place. This applies to "Open diaries in the editor", the preview's Edit and double-tap, and Edit in the right pane on wide windows. New diaries are unchanged.
- The keyboard doesn't pop up on open; tap the body to place the cursor there. While editing, the top bar shows "Done".
- Tap the info bar to open the details panel, and the page recolors right away when you change the mood. "More" has Edit details, Share, Export, Write a comment, AI reply, Set as anniversary, Save as template, Page style, Photos from this day, AI writing, Editor settings and Delete.
- The end of the page matches the preview: last edited time, diaries that mention this one, comments and AI replies.

### Thumbnails three to a row, cursor next to blocks
- Consecutive thumbnails in an entry sit three to a row, aligned left, center or right like the first one, and images inserted after a thumbnail are thumbnails too. PDF, Word and web exports lay them out three to a row as well.
- The cursor can rest on either side of images, videos, recordings, attachments, dividers and diary cards: tap the empty space beside one and the cursor goes to that side with the keyboard up, and typing or pressing Enter starts a new line there. When an entry starts with an image, you can now write above it.
- With the cursor right of a block, Backspace deletes it. On an empty line below a block, Backspace removes the line instead of just selecting the block.

### Search
- The search box is now a pill, with a scrollable row of filters below: tags, date, mood, images and attachments, weather, place and "Include archived".
- Multi-word search: separate words with spaces and every word must appear in the title, body or comments. Matches are highlighted, and when a match is further down, the snippet starts near it.
- Before you type, you see recent searches (delete them one by one or clear all) and common tags.
- Results show the count above them and can be sorted by recently updated, newest or oldest; the sort is remembered.
- "Save as smart notebook" carries over every word, tag and filter.
- On a computer, Esc clears the box before leaving, and Ctrl / ⌘ + F jumps back to it.

### App lock: re-locking and screenshot blocking
- In Settings → App lock & passcode, with app lock on, there are new options: "Lock after leaving" (immediately, after 1 / 5 / 15 / 30 minutes or 1 hour, or never; never is the default and keeps locking only when the app opens), "Lock when the screen locks" (Android), "Lock when idle" (computers, after 1–30 minutes without input) and "Ask right away" (on by default).
- A new Privacy group has "Block screenshots" (Android): screenshots, screen recordings and the recent apps view show only black, and it works without app lock too.
- Screens the app opens itself, such as picking photos, taking a picture, choosing files or the system share sheet, don't count as leaving. Coming back from inserting a photo in a locked notebook no longer asks you to verify again.
- When both app lock and a locked notebook need verifying, you verify once.
- With app lock on, loosening protection (locking later, turning off screen-lock or idle locking, turning off screenshot blocking) needs verification first.

### Other
- Cards for untitled diaries use the first line of the body as the title, and the snippet starts from the second line. Entries with only images still show "Untitled". The switch is in Settings → Appearance → Diary card style → Content: "Use the first line as the title when there is none" (on by default); if you had turned off "Untitled", the title line stays hidden.
- Home tags: tap to show only that tag, long-press to select several (Ctrl / ⌘ + click on a computer).
- Settings → Editor → Behavior adds "Publish new diaries when going back" (off by default): going back from a new diary publishes it instead of saving a draft. Leaving with nothing written still just leaves, and time capsules still go through the publish page to be sealed.
- Android: going back from a top-level notebook or "Not in a notebook" returns to the home screen, and the app reopens in the same notebook. Sub-notebooks still go up one level first.
- Chapters on a notebook's contents page can be dragged to reorder, and tapping a chapter name collapses or expands it. Dropping a diary into a collapsed chapter expands it.

## Improvements
- The keyboard opens and closes more smoothly, especially with the glass material: while editing an existing diary, each frame of the keyboard animation dropped from 15–25 ms to 3–8 ms.
- Drag handles share one style and the whole handle can be grabbed (tag management, toolbar, card sections, Explore layout, AI writing instructions, diary templates, notebook management and notebook contents); before, only the small icon itself responded.
- Selecting text in entries on phones is easier: the menu waits until you lift your finger so it doesn't cover the magnifier, selection handles are easier to grab, dragging a handle to the top or bottom edge scrolls, and dragging handles, copying or Select all no longer bring back a keyboard you closed.
- With many diaries, Explore computes its stats in the background and no longer stutters on open; writing full backups and diary packs moved off the UI thread too.
- Multi-turn AI chats replay earlier questions and tool calls exactly as sent, so prompt caching hits more often and long chats use fewer tokens.

## Fixes
- On HarmonyOS and some Huawei devices, installing an APK downloaded from GitHub said the package "contains no certificates". APKs now carry v1, v2 and v3 signatures with the same certificate, so you can update in place.
- Picking a place from "Recent places" often came without coordinates, and "Get weather" said there were none. It now takes the coordinates from the latest diary that has them.
- After closing the keyboard, tapping from one empty line to another didn't bring the keyboard back.
- AI chat: the toast covered the input box; tapping the input at the bottom of a chat left the last messages behind the keyboard; scrolling up while an answer was generating took a lot of force and kept snapping back to the bottom.
- Opening AI chat with a question could send the request before settings were loaded, so notebooks excluded in AI privacy weren't excluded.
- Reopening an AI chat pinned to a diary didn't restore that pinned scope.
- `%` and `_` in search terms were treated as wildcards.
- Shrinking the window on a computer made an expanded toolbar panel in the editor overflow.

## Notes
- This version upgrades the database (v18 → v21): two new tables for to-dos and one new column each for AI messages and chats (tool steps, model and thinking level). Existing diaries are not changed.
- LAN sync now includes to-dos, so its data format changes: both devices need this version to keep syncing. With different versions you'll see "Incompatible versions" and nothing is written.
- Restoring a backup from this version with an older version leaves out the to-dos.
- To-do reminders need notification permission; Android 13 and later asks the first time you set a reminder. On phones the system schedules them, so they fire even when the app isn't running and are rescheduled after a restart. On computers they only fire while the app is running.
- To-dos and the AI assistant are both off by default; turn them on in Settings → Features.
- Thinking levels are sent the way each provider currently expects; providers that don't accept them automatically fall back to a request without a level.
- On Android you can install over the current version, or update in the app from Settings → About → Check for updates.
