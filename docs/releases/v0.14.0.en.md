# Release v0.14.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.14.0.md)

## New
- Block selection: tap a block's handle to select several blocks, then convert or style them in one go, move, copy or delete them together, or split crowded text into paragraphs and merge blocks into one.
- Edits to existing diaries can be saved manually (Settings → Editor → Save edits manually).
- The AI assistant can look at photos in your diaries. You can send photos or take one in chat, and have the AI add them to a diary.
- Redesigned AI chat: the input bar moves extras into a "+" menu, a scroll-to-bottom button, answers that type out at a steady pace, and proper code blocks and tables.

## Improvements
- Pasting multi-line text from the keyboard's clipboard puts each line in its own paragraph.
- The selection menu follows the text as it scrolls and no longer covers the toolbar panel; opening the panel lifts selected text it would hide.

## Fixes
- Compressing videos in Storage crashed on Android 7–11.
- Long-pressing text and dragging to the top of the editor froze the app; it now scrolls up.
- The AI assistant said it couldn't find the text of diaries it tried to read.
- A "Send to …?" confirmation popped up after each answer in a new AI chat.

## Notes
- Restoring a backup from this version with an older version leaves out photos sent in AI chats.
