# Release v0.3.0+6

English | [简体中文](./v0.3.0+6.md)

## Highlights
- Added Time Capsules for writing diary entries to your future self.
- Capsules stay sealed until the chosen date, showing only countdown plus the mood, weather, and energy from that moment.
- After unlock, a warm insight connects how you felt when writing with how you feel today.

## Added
- Added a time lock entry in the diary publish panel, with future date or exact time selection.
- Added a minimal lock animation when sealing a capsule.
- Added locked cards on the home timeline: text and images stay blurred, with an “unlocks in …” countdown in the center.
- Locked cards keep the original mood, weather, and energy visible around the edges.
- Added a locked detail page for viewing countdown and metadata, changing unlock time, or deleting the capsule.
- Time Capsules appear on the calendar day they unlock, without showing text or images before that date.
- Locked capsules stay hidden from body search and media recap paths before unlock.
- Deleting a Time Capsule now supports undo.
