# Release v0.3.0+6

English | [简体中文](./v0.3.0+6.md)

## Highlights
- Added Time Capsules for writing diary entries to your future self.
- Keeps sealed content hidden across the home timeline and calendar review until it unlocks.
- Preserves the weather, mood, and energy from the sealing moment for richer context later.

## Added
- Added a time lock entry in the diary publish panel, with future date or exact time selection.
- Added a capsule sealing flow with a minimal lock animation and placement on the unlock date.
- Added locked timeline cards that blur text and images while showing countdown, mood, weather, and energy.
- Added a locked detail page for viewing countdown and metadata, changing unlock time, or deleting the capsule.
- Added a warm unlock insight at the top of diary details after a capsule becomes readable.

## Changed
- Time Capsule publish dates now use the unlock date, so capsules appear on the day they open.
- Calendar day timelines now show locked state only for sealed capsules, without exposing text or images.
- Locked capsules no longer expose body text through search or media recap paths before unlock.
- Deleting a locked capsule now supports undo, matching normal diary deletion behavior.
- Refined masonry countdown copy and font size for better readability in narrow cards.

## Fixed
- Fixed time lock picker overflow on small screens by moving it into the floating publish panel.
- Fixed horizontal overflow in narrow masonry locked capsule cards.
- Fixed calendar day timelines exposing sealed capsule body text before unlock.
