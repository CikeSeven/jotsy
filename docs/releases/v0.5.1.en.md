# Release v0.5.1

English | [简体中文](./v0.5.1.md)

- Added diary voice recording and playback, with pause/resume, renaming, and seekable waveforms.
- Added videos and file attachments, with fullscreen video playback and saving to the gallery.
- Insert multiple images at once and choose whether to record an energy level when publishing a diary.
- Refreshed the Material 3 Expressive interface, including cards, tags, typography, and light/dark themes.
- Home tag filters now expand and scroll; improved diary cover sizes and aspect ratios.
- Fixed media import and backup restoration issues, improved backups of recordings, videos, attachments, and drafts, and reduced memory use when backing up or restoring large files.
- Fixed older backups being rejected when they contain leftover links to deleted tags or diaries, preserving all existing diaries, tags, and media during restoration.
- Fixed tag deletion failing when tag preferences are unset, preserving diary contents and the remaining tags.
