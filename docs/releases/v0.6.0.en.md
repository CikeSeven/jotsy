# Release v0.6.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.6.0.md)

## Highlights
- Cloud backup: back up to WebDAV (presets for Jianguoyun, Nextcloud, Synology and more), S3-compatible object storage (Aliyun OSS, Tencent COS, Cloudflare R2 and more), Google Drive and Dropbox, then list, restore and delete cloud backups inside the app.
- Import from other apps: Day One, Journey, Diarium, Daylio, Apple Journal, Evernote, Google Keep, Joplin, Memos, Diaro and Markdown / plain text, plus signing in to Xiaomi, Huawei, OPPO, vivo, HONOR, Meizu and WPS to read notes straight from their cloud.
- A much richer calendar: lunar dates and solar terms, legal holidays and make-up workdays, anniversaries and countdown days, a year view, customizable day cells and stats for a date range.
- Deep customization: diary card styles, preview page styles (presets, saved styles and style codes) and the body font and layout.
- Sharing and export: one tap to Weibo, Xiaohongshu, WeChat, WeChat Moments, QQ and Qzone; export to PDF, Word, Markdown, web page, plain text or CSV, from a multi-selection or a date range.

## New
- Anniversaries: see anniversaries and countdown days on the calendar, with a countdown and the diaries written on each past occurrence on the detail page; link a diary, or turn a diary into an anniversary from the preview page.
- The preview page swipes to the previous or next diary. Long images are folded and open at screen width, or show in full if you change the reading setting. Tapping a body image and choosing "View" opens it from where it sits, with double-tap zoom and pull-down to close.
- When writing a diary for a past day, a strip under the title shows the photos you took that day, ready to insert or set as the cover.
- Editor tools that do not fit move into a More panel; the publish time of a published diary can be edited; creating a diary from a future calendar date presets the time capsule's unlock day.
- Choose how detailed the location is when publishing (city, district, township, road or house number); weather is fetched once the location is found; the collapsed publish panel shows a summary bar with a publish button.
- Sort diaries by publish time in either direction; cards can hide the "Untitled" placeholder.
- Export a single diary as a diary file; when importing a backup, choose to merge or overwrite, with a strong warning before overwriting.
- Tap the current home tab again to scroll back to the top.

## Improvements
- One motion rhythm across the app that honors the system "reduce motion" setting; shared-element transitions between the image viewer and covers, thumbnails and body images; light haptics when an action takes effect.
- At launch, the system splash hands over seamlessly to the in-app brand mark and the home screen unfolds; when data is ready quickly, the home screen shows directly instead of a fixed loading page.
- The editor stays smooth while the keyboard opens or the panel is dragged; lists, tag filters and calendar switches no longer flicker or jump; sealed time capsule cards scroll more smoothly.
- Redesigned recording panel and player: a live scrolling voiceprint and a main button that changes shape with the state.
- Swipe-to-archive in the diary list follows your finger and gives a light haptic past the threshold; in multi-select the top bar stays pinned and shows the selected count.
- Body images render as their own blocks (rounded corners, spacing above and below); shrunk images align to the line start.
- Word count follows the same rules as Word and WPS, everywhere in the app.
- The create button shows on the diaries tab only; weather icons follow the theme color.
- Accessibility: screen readers no longer read diary content while the app is locked; color swatches have 48dp touch targets; icon-only buttons have labels.
- No leftover Chinese text in the English interface.

## Fixes
- The editor now works with phone keyboards: the keyboard's "select" mode selects text again, and when you type at the bottom or tap text near the bottom to bring up the keyboard, the content moves up with the keyboard and keeps a gap above the toolbar.
- You can no longer go back or publish twice while a diary is being published, which could leave the draft in the editor after the diary was already saved; a new diary's publish time no longer freezes at the moment the publish page opened.
- Editing a published diary no longer drops its time capsule details; the capsule picker follows its presets and precision; new diaries leave energy unrecorded by default.
- Location falls back to system location when a request fails, and address lookup no longer has a coordinate offset.
- Fixed a black flash during launch.
- Fixed mood and weather stamps missing on list cards, distorted calendar thumbnails, and "next month" not turning the page in the calendar's week view.
- The toolbar order page asks before discarding unsaved changes instead of dropping them silently.
- Removing items in the archive and recycle bin now collapses them smoothly instead of jumping.

## Notes
This release adds a lot. Diaries, backup files and existing settings remain compatible, so you can upgrade with a direct overwrite install. The new card styles, preview page styles, body layout, calendar display and share and export options are all included in backups.
