# Release v0.7.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.7.0.md)

## Highlights
- Notebooks: sort diaries into notebooks, with one diary in several at once. Chapters and book order, smart notebooks, hidden and locked notebooks, stats and writing goals, and export a whole notebook as a book.
- Diary templates and drafts: 8 built-in templates, your own templates or any diary saved as one, applied automatically by time of day. Every new entry is its own draft, so "New blank" no longer wipes out the last one.
- Features: turn off Calendar, Explore, notebooks, anniversaries, diary templates or time capsules for a cleaner app. Your data stays as it is.
- A new color panel in the editor: pick colors right in the toolbar, choose any color from a spectrum, type a color code, and reuse recent colors.
- More complete imports from other apps: colors, text sizes, alignment, dividers and all kinds of attachments are kept. Xiaomi notes with images, and some Meizu and vivo notes, are no longer silently skipped, and notes that are skipped are listed with a reason in the preview.

## New
### Notebooks
- The first chip in the home tag bar switches notebooks. Pick one and the home page turns into that notebook, with an overview card at the top; diaries you create there go into it.
- A diary can be in several notebooks, and removing it from a notebook does not delete it. Add, remove or move diaries from multi-select, the publish panel, the editor and the preview page.
- Chapters and book order: split a notebook into chapters and drag to arrange the whole book on its contents page.
- Smart notebooks collect diaries by tags, mood, date, text, images and more, and can start from an example. Save search results as a smart notebook, or a date range picked on the calendar as a notebook; a smart notebook can be turned into a regular one.
- Hidden and locked notebooks: diaries in a private notebook stay out of All diaries, search, Calendar, Explore and exports. A locked notebook asks you to verify before opening, editing or deleting it, and locks again when the app goes to the background.
- Stats and writing goals. A notebook can have a default template that is applied to new diaries written in it.
- Export as book (notebook ⋮ menu → Export as book): PDF, Word, web page, Markdown and plain text follow book order with a title page, contents and chapters; the PDF contents show page numbers and link to each entry.
- Filter the calendar by notebook. Manage notebooks in Settings → Notebooks.

### Diary templates
- 8 built-in templates: Daily journal, Morning pages, Evening review, Gratitude journal, Question of the day, Weekly review, Untangle a feeling and Reading notes. Copy one to make it your own.
- When you start a blank diary, templates for the time of day are suggested under the title. Tap one to apply it, with 4 seconds to undo; leaving without writing anything does not create a draft.
- Templates support 12 variables such as `{{date}}`, `{{weekday}}`, `{{lunar}}` and `{{term}}`, plus preset tags, mood, energy and a time capsule.
- Apply automatically by time range: ranges can cross midnight and be limited to certain weekdays, with a default template for other times.
- Choose a template, or switch to another, from the editor ⋮ menu; save a diary as a template from the preview page or the editor (images and other media are left out). The template picker has search and category filters.
- Share templates as template codes and import them from the clipboard. Manage templates in Settings → Editor → Diary templates.

### Drafts
- Every new diary is its own draft, saved automatically when you go back or switch away, so the last few seconds of typing are no longer lost.
- With drafts waiting, New lists the latest ones: continue a draft, Start from a template, or start a blank one.
- The Drafts page (home top bar, and Settings → Data & Privacy): swipe left to delete, long-press to select several; both can be undone.

### More
- Settings → Features: turn off Calendar (anniversaries go with it), Explore, notebooks, anniversaries, diary templates and time capsules. Turning a feature off only hides its entries and automatic behavior; your data is kept and comes back when you turn it on again.
- Color panel: text and background colors open inside the floating toolbar, with no dialog and no keyboard flicker. Swatches holds presets and recent colors; Custom has a spectrum, background opacity and a color code field.
- "Photos from this day" in the editor ⋮ menu opens the photos taken that day, for both new and existing diaries. The editor setting is now "Show that day's photos automatically" and only controls the automatic suggestion when backfilling.
- Tags in the publish panel: search by name or pinyin (full or initials), create a tag straight from the search, and sort alphabetically with an A–Z bar on the right to jump around.
- Image viewer: long-press an image or tap ⋮ to share it straight to installed apps such as WeChat or Weibo, or save it to the gallery. From the Explore media gallery you can also open the diary it belongs to.
- Exports follow your body font: with an imported font selected, PDFs embed it, and Word and web pages refer to it by name.

## Improvements
- The home top bar drops its title for a wider search bar. With no tags, the empty tag bar is hidden, and the tag row sits closer to the search bar.
- The share button in the preview page title bar now opens the share sheet, same as More → Share.
- Short "more" menus, such as the editor top bar and the anniversary details page, are now anchored popup menus with one style across the app.
- Radio buttons, icons and arrows stay centered when an option's description wraps.
- Dividers from imported notes show as a thin line and export as a divider in every format; small, large and huge text now scales with the body text size.

## Fixes
- Xiaomi cloud notes with images were skipped entirely; Meizu notes with content the importer did not fully recognize were skipped; text after an image or divider in vivo notes was lost.
- Notes skipped by cloud imports (encrypted, handwriting and other proprietary types, unrecognized) are now counted by reason in the import preview. When a long WPS note body cannot be read, its summary is imported instead of failing the whole import.
- File imports from other apps: fixed encoding, date, attachment path and format issues in several sources; shared attachments and duplicate entries are no longer lost.
- Scrolling fast in the home waterfall view jumped back near the top when the next page loaded.
- The Undo message after deleting a diary on the calendar and the View message after creating an anniversary never went away, so the undo window never ended and the home button stayed raised.

## Notes
- On first launch the database is upgraded with new tables for drafts, templates and notebooks; existing diaries are not changed. An unfinished new diary from the previous version shows up in Drafts.
- Full backups now include notebooks, templates and drafts. Overwriting everything with an older backup that has no templates clears your templates, and the warning shows how many; merging only adds templates you don't have and never replaces yours.
- Like the app lock, locked notebooks rely on the system screen lock; without a screen lock, locked notebooks cannot be opened.
- Word and web exports made with an imported font do not embed it, so it only shows on devices that have the font installed.
- Diaries, backup files and existing settings remain compatible, so you can upgrade with a direct overwrite install or update inside the app from Settings → About → Check for updates.
