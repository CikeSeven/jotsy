# Release v0.8.1

English | [简体中文](./v0.8.1.md)

## Highlights
- Refreshed built-in templates: all 8 have been rewritten with colored bold headings at body size instead of large titles that filled the screen. Built-in templates can now be edited and deleted, and an edited one can always be restored to the original.
- Select all in Drafts and the recycle bin. The recycle bin has been redesigned with text snippets and covers, and a bottom bar to restore or permanently delete what you select.
- More faithful exports: headings in PDF exports keep their colors and formatting, and Word exports follow your body text size, line height, letter spacing and paragraph spacing.

## New
### Diary templates
- All 8 built-in templates have been rewritten. Headings start with an emoji and use colored bold text at body size; Morning pages shows your wake-up time and solar term, Weekly review the week number and how far through the year you are, in small grey text; sections are separated by a blank line. Text you write after a heading stays plain.
- Edit built-in templates directly: tap one to open the editor. Saving replaces the original in place in the built-in list, marked "Edited"; recent use, auto-apply by time range and notebook default templates keep pointing at it. "Restore original" can be undone.
- Delete built-in templates: a deleted one leaves the management page, the template picker and the suggestions. "Restore deleted built-ins" at the end of the management page brings them back one by one or all at once, along with any edits made before deleting.
- With built-ins editable, "Copy to my templates" and "Hide" are gone.

### Drafts and recycle bin
- Select all: in Drafts, "Select all" sits next to delete while selecting, and tapping it again deselects everything and leaves selection mode. In the recycle bin, "Select all" is in the top bar whenever there are diaries.
- The recycle bin is now a list of cards with a two-line snippet, a cover thumbnail and "date · deleted at". Diaries from private notebooks show only a placeholder title and a lock; sealed time capsules show only their title and date.
- Selecting diaries raises a bottom bar with Restore and Permanent delete. While selecting, the top-left button becomes a close button, and system back clears the selection first.

## Fixes
- Headings in PDF exports lost their text color, background color, weight, underline and other formatting as well as links, and differently formatted text on one line came out the same.
- Word exports did not follow the size, line height, letter spacing and paragraph spacing set in Body font & layout; lists and quotes follow them now too.
- In the tens of minutes after a new version is published while its APKs are still uploading, a manual update check showed "check failed". It now says you're up to date and offers the new version once the APKs are uploaded.

## Notes
- No database upgrade in this version.
- Built-in templates hidden in v0.8.0 count as deleted after the upgrade and no longer appear in the management list. Bring them back from "Restore deleted built-ins" at the end of Settings → Editor → Diary templates.
- The new built-in template styles only apply to diaries you start from them from now on; diaries you already wrote are unchanged. Edited built-in templates are included in full backups and restored when overwriting; merging still only adds templates this device doesn't have and never replaces yours.
- Diaries, backup files and existing settings remain compatible, so you can upgrade with a direct overwrite install or update inside the app from Settings → About → Check for updates.
