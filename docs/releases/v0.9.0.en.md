# Release v0.9.0

English | [简体中文](./v0.9.0.md)

## Highlights
- Desktop is here: the first Windows and Linux builds. Wide windows get a side navigation rail and a list + reading two-pane Diaries page, with right-click menus, Ctrl / Shift multi-select, keyboard shortcuts and drag-and-drop files into the editor.
- A new editor: diaries are now written in a block editor, with Markdown shortcuts, an inline `/` command menu next to the cursor, drag-to-move paragraphs with a live preview, and nested indentation. Lists get a choice of numbering and bullets, the toolbar can be resized, and body layout adds a first-line indent.
- Background & Material: set a background color or image for the whole app or for each page, and turn top bars, the toolbar, sheets, dialogs, cards and controls into frosted glass, together or one by one.
- Explore, rebuilt: 21 cards you can reorder, show or hide, resize and configure, with This week / This month / This year / All time at the top. 5 built-in layouts, plus your own layouts saved or shared as layout codes.
- Notebooks can hold sub-notebooks and have custom covers, and the shelf gets "Not in a notebook" to find diaries you haven't filed yet.
- New: write comments on diaries you reread, and record the mood of the day on the calendar. Plus imports from Durian Diary (榴莲日记) and Smartisan Notes.

## New
### Windows and Linux
- Packages: an installer and a portable zip for Windows 10 or later (x64), and a tarball with `install.sh` for Linux (x64, GTK 3). See the README or the website for downloads and setup.
- Wide-window layouts (by window width, so tablets in landscape benefit too): from 600 wide a side navigation rail replaces the bottom bar; from 1024 the Diaries page splits into a list and a reading pane. Click a card to read it on the right; the card you're reading gets an accent ring. The editor, preview and settings pages keep their content in a centered column instead of stretching across the window.
- Mouse: right-click a diary card to edit, pin, file into a notebook, archive or delete it; Ctrl / ⌘ + click to multi-select, Shift + click to select a range; drag horizontal strips, or Shift + scroll to move them sideways; scroll long images up and down, Ctrl + scroll to zoom.
- Keyboard: Ctrl / ⌘ + N for a new diary, F to search, 1–4 to switch pages; in the two-pane view ↑ / ↓ to move between diaries, Enter to edit, Delete to delete (with undo), Esc to clear the selection; ← / → to page through the preview and image viewer, Esc to go back.
- Editing: drag images, videos or files from your file manager into the editor to insert them at the cursor. The floating toolbar stays visible, and the usual shortcuts (Ctrl + B / I / Z) work.
- Unsaved changes are saved before the window closes, and the window stays open if saving fails. Launching the app again brings the open window to the front.
- Updates: you're told when a new version is out, and "Go to download" opens your browser to fetch the package for your platform over the mirror routes.
- Phone only for now: sharing to a specific app, cloud note imports from phone makers and WPS, "Photos from this day", and automatic location and weather. On Windows and Linux, videos in a diary open in the system player; the Linux build doesn't support voice recording, app lock or locked notebooks yet.

### A new editor
- The editor, preview and templates all use the same block editor. Markdown shortcuts (`# `, `- `, `> `, `1. ` and so on) work on the on-screen keyboard too.
- `/` commands open an inline menu next to the cursor; keep typing to filter (pinyin works too, e.g. `/biaoti`), and the keyboard stays up.
- Paragraph handle: tap the handle beside the current paragraph to move it up or down, or choose "Hold to drag". While you drag, the paragraph moves into place live and keeps its indentation; one undo puts it back.
- List styles: with the cursor in a list, tap the list tool to open the list panel. Numbered lists: 1. / (1) / ① / 一、/ a. / A. / i. / I.; bulleted lists: filled dot, hollow circle, square, diamond, star, arrow or dash. The whole list changes at once, and exports keep the style.
- Toolbar size: Settings → Editor → Toolbar size — Small, Standard, Large or Extra large.
- First-line indent: Settings → Editor → Body font & layout (also in the preview page's layout panel), 0–4 characters, off by default. Only plain paragraphs are indented, and Word exports indent the same way.
- Open diaries in the editor: in Settings → Editor. When on, opening a diary from anywhere goes straight to the editor (time capsules that are still sealed show their sealed page first).

### Background & Material
- Settings → Appearance & Language → Background & Material. Set one global background, or choose Follow global, None or Custom for each of 7 areas: Diaries, Calendar, Explore, Settings, Editor, Preview and Other. Backgrounds can be a color or an image, with framing, blur, and separate light and dark overlays.
- Component style: switch everything between Classic and Glass in one go, or set Page top bars, Editor toolbar, Bottom sheets, Dialogs, Cards & groups and Controls & toasts separately. Glass blur, opacity, tint and outline are adjustable.
- Diary card style adds a Glass material and preset. The style editors for diary cards, the navigation bar, the preview page and code blocks now preview on top of that page's background.
- Background images are included in full backups.

### Explore
- 21 cards in four groups: Memories & ideas (Today, On this day, Random memory, Recap, Milestones), Charts (Dashboard, Writing heatmap, Writing rhythm, Writing habits, Mood trend, Mood breakdown, Energy trend), Life log (Tags, Media gallery, Places, Weather) and Linked features (Anniversaries, Time capsules, Notebooks, Drafts, Recent comments).
- The period bar at the top — This week, This month, This year, All time — recalculates the cards that follow it.
- Customize from the button in Explore's top bar or Settings → Appearance & Language → Explore page. Drag cards to reorder, switch them on or off, make them half or full width and adjust each card's options. Appearance covers density, corner radius, title style, card surface, accent color and the number of columns in wide windows. Built-in layouts: Balanced, Minimal, Data lover, Memories and Everything. The preview switches between your data and sample data.
- Long-press a card (right-click on desktop) to open its settings or hide it (with undo).

### Notebooks
- Sub-notebooks: up to three levels, and a parent shows every diary in its sub-notebooks. Parents with children appear as a stack on the shelf; the home header lists sub-notebooks and shows the parent path, and Back takes you up one level. A sub-notebook inside a hidden or locked parent is private as well.
- Custom covers: 5 layouts (Classic, Full bleed, Spine, Label, Minimal) and 4 textures (Dots, Lines, Grid, Diagonal). Drag and zoom to frame the cover image, or pick one straight from the diaries in the notebook.
- The shelf adds "Not in a notebook" under All diaries, listing only diaries that aren't in any notebook; All diaries now shows the total count.
- Exporting a parent as a book gives each sub-notebook its own chapter, and filtering the calendar by a parent includes its sub-notebooks.

### Comments
- Settings → Features → Comments (off by default). Then use More → Write a comment on the preview page to respond to a diary, optionally with a mood. Comments line up after the text in order, labeled "N days later", and can be edited, copied or deleted (with undo). The diary text itself never changes.
- Diary cards and the info bar show the comment count; search matches comments too; Explore gets a Recent comments card; and exports can include comments.
- Shared text and long images don't include comments.

### Mood of the day
- Long-press a day on the calendar to record how the day went (Awful, Bad, Meh, Good, Great), even on days without a diary. The date cell fills with the mood's color and face, and the year view uses it first when coloring by mood.
- It's separate from each diary's own mood and can be hidden in the calendar display options.

### Imports
- Durian Diary (榴莲日记): import the `.lld` file from its zip export, or its exported folder zipped up. Text, images, videos, attachments, mood, weather, location, tags and notebooks come along, and follow-up notes are added at the end.
- Smartisan Notes: sign in to Smartisan Cloud in the app for a read-only import. The sign-in is used only for this import and cleared when it ends.

### More
- A fast scroller on the Diaries list: it appears at the right edge while you scroll; hold and drag to jump to any month (initials when sorted by title, chapters in a notebook's book order), however many diaries you have.
- Settings → Features adds a Swipe to archive switch, for anyone who'd rather turn the gesture off.
- The color panel's swatches now include pure white.

## Improvements
- Publishing takes one step less: tap Publish in the editor and the diary is published. Anything you add afterwards on the publish page — cover, tags, mood, time capsule and so on — saves as you go, and the top bar shows "✓ Diary published" instead of a snackbar.
- Swipe to archive only triggers if you've passed the threshold when you let go, so a quick flick no longer archives by accident, and the revealed background follows the card's rounded corners.
- On the preview page, Edit now sits next to More, swapping places with Share.
- List bullets, checkboxes and code line numbers line up exactly with the first line of text, and headings scale with the body font size.
- Layout changes such as paragraph spacing and heading size apply right away to an open editor and the settings preview, without reopening.

## Fixes
- Importing a video on Android crashed on some devices when permission handling failed or the picker returned an error.
- PDF exports merged differently formatted text in headings and body, losing color, weight, underline and links.
- Indented sub-paragraphs disappeared after their parent was turned into a heading, quote or code block; existing diaries like this show them again when opened.
- Selection handles on the preview page wouldn't move when dragged slowly, and tapping read-only text left a cursor behind.
- Resizing an image had no live preview.

## Notes
- **This version upgrades the database (v13 → v16) and switches to a new format for diary text. Don't downgrade after upgrading**: older versions can't read the new format, and editing there may overwrite content. Backups made with this version can't be restored in older versions either. We recommend making a full backup first in Settings → Data & Privacy → Data Management.
- Existing diaries open and edit normally with no conversion step, and are saved in the new format. Template codes move to v2; v1 codes still import.
- New defaults: on a fresh install, Anniversaries and Comments are off (if you've already created anniversaries, they stay on after upgrading). Word count and reading time in the preview info bar are hidden by default; turn them on under Info in the preview page style.
- Background & Material, the Explore layout, moods of the day, comments and notebook nesting are included in full backups. Restoring an older backup without them leaves your current settings as they are. Toolbar size is a per-device preference and isn't backed up.
- The desktop builds aren't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher on first run; you can check the files against `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
- On Android, install over the existing app to upgrade, or update in the app from Settings → About → Check for updates.
