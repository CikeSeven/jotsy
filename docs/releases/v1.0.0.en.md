# Release v1.0.0

English | [简体中文](./v1.0.0.md)

## New
- Mind maps: a mind map is a diary you edit on a canvas or as an outline, and can also be embedded in a diary. Import and export them, turn a text diary into one, or have the AI generate, expand and summarize them.
- Android home screen widgets: quick capture, today, to-do list, anniversaries, mood calendar, on this day, recent diaries, writing stats and photo memories, styled in the widget studio.
- Find and replace: search and replace text while editing (regex supported), and find text in the preview and in search results.
- Export as an EPUB e-book.
- Comments support bold and other formats, plus links to diaries, notebooks and web pages.
- Obsidian [[ ]] links: imported notes keep their links as diary links, and typing [[ in a diary inserts one.
- Recording keeps going with the screen off or the app in the background; imported audio plays right in the diary.
- Videos can show as thumbnails and be resized, with a redesigned full-screen player that supports pinch to zoom.
- The media gallery in Explore groups photos by date, switches columns with a pinch, and filters by notebook and time.
- Diary cards can show their notebooks (turn it on in card style), and can skip using the first photo as the cover.
- The AI assistant can manage diary templates.

## Improvements
- The navigation bar is now a floating glass pill with adjustable roundness and items; home tabs are fixed as Diaries, To-dos, Calendar, Explore.
- Settings, drafts and archive move into the "More" menu on the Diaries tab.
- Redesigned popup menus: more compact, and you can slide your finger to pick an item.
- Diary cards expand into the preview and shrink back on return; switching tabs and tags has a directional transition.
- Smoother tab swiping on the home page, smoother Explore, and smoother scrolling with glass surfaces.
- Top bar buttons in the preview and editor stay in fixed places, and "More" opens a popup menu.
- To-do priorities use bold colors, and to-dos sort by priority by default.
- Global search includes diaries in notebooks hidden from All Diaries.
- Every notebook picker can collapse and expand sub-notebooks.
- Unused files in Storage can be cleaned right away, or viewed and deleted one by one.
- Outdenting can cancel the global first-line indent for just that paragraph.

## Fixes
- Emoji printed as boxes in PDFs.
- With settings sync on, imported fonts were switched back to HarmonyOS Sans.
- Weather entered by hand showed no icon on diary cards.
- Untitled diaries in a notebook's contents didn't show their first line.
- Enter stopped adding new lines after leaving block selection; clicking at the start of a line on desktop entered block selection by mistake.
- Pinching a photo in the viewer could flip pages or close it.
- To-do reminders couldn't be set after today; to-dos created in the planned view didn't show up.

## Notes
- LAN sync requires both devices to be on this version.
- Restoring a backup from this version with an older version drops comment formatting, and mind maps can't be displayed.
